# Avasin Standard — Laki Appendices 397–400
**Status:** Test-branch research content, not yet production-published.

## Scope
- 397: causal conjunctions — `ki`, `čü(n) (ki)`, `ařā (ya ki)`, `bi xātir (ya ki)`.
- 398: purpose clauses — `ki`, `tā`, infinitive `-(i)n`.
- 399: temporal clauses — `ki`, `tā`, `iska (ki)`, `baʕd (a ya) ki`, `vaxtī (ki)`, `zamānī (ki)`.
- 400: conditional clauses — `a(ga)r`, Protasis/Apodosis, realizable and counterfactual conditionals.

## Research safeguards
- Main reference: Sara Belelli (2021), *The Laki Variety of Harsin: Grammar, Texts, Lexicon*, DOI: https://doi.org/10.20378/irb-51703.
- Harsini is a specific variety; do not generalize these findings to all Laki varieties.
- Preserve all Latin-script examples exactly as transcribed in the source.
- Appendix 397: no independent corpus example was found for `ařā (ya ki)` in the reviewed passage; do not invent one.
- Appendix 398–399: `tā` is polyfunctional; distinguish purpose, temporal and degree readings by context.
- Appendix 400: the source leaves open whether Imperfective can replace Past Subjunctive in all counterfactual protases.
- This test page is a research appendix index, not a complete published Laki curriculum.
