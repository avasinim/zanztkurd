# Kurmancî Medium B3 — gihandin and şibandin: lexical stems, transitivity, and past evidence audit

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only lexical and grammatical audit. No lesson text, course inventory, runtime, CI, seed, database, migration, PR, or deployment changes.

## Executive findings

| Infinitive | Meaning | Transitivity | Present stem | Past stem | 1sg present | Secure past evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gihandin | to deliver, convey, bring/reach something to a destination | transitive | gihîn- | gihand- | ez digihînim | min gihand (3sg patient); literary plural clause cited below |
| şibandin | to liken, compare, mistake one person for another | transitive | şibîn- | şiband- | ez dişibînim | min tu şibandî kesekî din |

Do not confuse **gihandin** (transitive “deliver/convey”) with **gihiştin** (intransitive “arrive/reach”). Likewise, keep **şibîn** (“resemble, look like”, intransitive) distinct from **şibandin** (“liken/compare”, transitive).

## 1. gihandin

### Stems and present paradigm

The Kurmancî Wiktionary entry explicitly labels gihandin transitive and gives present stem **gihîn-**, past stem **gihand-**, and:
- ez digihînim
- tu digihînî
- ew digihîne
- em/hûn/ew digihînin
- singular imperative bigihîne; plural bigihînin

The Ich lerne Kurmancî verb list independently gives gihandin <-gihîn->, ez digihînim, bigihîne, gihand. Kurmanci.app's Ferheng describes it as a transitive verb and records the sense “to deliver, convey, bring/reach to a place,” with lexical examples including **Wana cab gihandine dîwana Al-paşa** and **Peyxemberan peyama aştî û biratiyê gihand tevahiya mirovan**.

### Simple past and literary evidence

The Wiktionary paradigm lists the past base **gihand** with oblique agents min, te, wî/wê, me, we, wan; this is the standard 3sg-patient shape. The entry also cites Sidqî Hirorî, *Kurê Zinarê Serbilind* (Nûdem, Stockholm, 1996): **Mirzayî destê Besnayê girt û wan jî zû xwe gihande rexê din cem hevalan.** The literary token **gihande** occurs in a clause with plural oblique agent **wan** and reflexive **xwe**; because the reflexive's agreement/case behavior is special, do not treat this as an ordinary plural-object agreement paradigm.

A classical citation from Melayê Cizîrî (~1640) contains **Kullab-i gihandin dil û hestî herişandin**. This is literary evidence for the lexical family, but its poetic morphology and syntax should not be presented as a modern simple-past model without a specialist reading.

### Contrast with gihiştin

The related intransitive verb **gihiştin** has present stem **gih-** and past stem **gihişt-** in the consulted learner list; Kurdish Academy gives **ez digihîjim**, **tu digihîjî**, **ew digihîje**, **em/hûn/ew digihîjin**, with past forms **gihîşt / gihîştin**. This is not an alternative conjugation of transitive gihandin:
- gihandin: someone delivers/conveys something — transitive
- gihiştin: someone/something arrives/reaches — intransitive

The two verbs must not be merged in a teaching table.

## 2. şibandin

### Stems, meaning, and present forms

Kurmanci.app's Ferheng labels **şibandin** transitive, gives the present stem **şibîn-**, and defines it as “liken/compare; mistake one person for another.” It cites Faik Şahin's *Ferhenga Kurdî–Tirkî*. The Ich lerne Kurmancî verb list independently gives:
- infinitive şibandin
- present stem şibîn-
- ez dişibînim
- imperative bişibîne
- past form şiband

This is a causative/transitivized lexical counterpart to **şibîn**, which is intransitive “resemble/look like” and has present stem **şib-** (ez dişibim; tu dişibî). Chyet's dictionary entry for şibîn records the intransitive construction and notes the related form şibandin / şibihandin. Keep the lexical distinction explicit.

### Past example and agreement

Faik Şahin's Ferheng gives the direct example:
**Min tu şibandî kesekî din, lewma min ji dûr ve gazî te kir.**
“I mistook/likened you to another person, so I called you from a distance.”

This is a valuable sourced example of a transitive past clause: oblique agent **min**, direct patient **tu**, and verb form **şibandî**. It directly supports the second-person singular patient form in this lexical item. The Ich lerne Kurmancî table confirms the stem pair and the 1sg present form, but does not supply a full six-person past object-agreement paradigm.

Do not invent the remaining patient-agreement forms from the single attested example. The general Kurmanji ergative rule is well documented, but verb-specific paradigms should be sourced or reviewed before memorization drills are written.

## 3. Alignment, negation, and aspect guardrails

For ordinary Standard Kurmancî transitive past clauses, the agent is oblique, the patient is direct, and the verb agrees with the patient. Haig & Öpengin (2018) and the University of Arizona Kurmanji overview describe this alignment, while noting that some spoken subvarieties show erosion or restructuring.

The forms **min gihand** and the lexical past base **şiband** are 3sg-patient citation forms. **Min tu şibandî** is a directly sourced 2sg-patient example. Do not extrapolate a complete paradigm without additional evidence. No verb-specific negative paradigm was independently confirmed in this audit; do not add constructed negatives as quoted or attested examples.

## 4. Evidence grading and teaching recommendation

- **High confidence:** gihandin is transitive; present stem gihîn-, past stem gihand-; complete present paradigm and imperative are explicitly listed.
- **High confidence:** şibandin is transitive; present stem şibîn-, past stem şiband; 1sg present and one direct 2sg-patient past example are explicitly documented.
- **High confidence:** gihandin and gihiştin are separate transitive/intransitive verbs with different stems and meanings.
- **High confidence:** şibandin and şibîn are separate transitive/intransitive lexemes; do not conflate “liken” with “resemble”.
- **Literary evidence:** Sidqî Hirorî (1996) gives a plural-agent clause with xwe; Melayê Cizîrî (~1640) has a poetic lexical attestation. Neither should be used to claim a full contemporary object-agreement paradigm.
- **Not established here:** full non-3sg patient paradigms for gihandin; full patient-agreement table for şibandin; a verb-specific negative paradigm for either verb.
- **Teaching recommendation:** teach the lexical contrasts and verified present forms first. Use **Min tu şibandî kesekî din** as the sourced past example for şibandin. Teach gihandin vs gihiştin as a transitive/intransitive contrast. Hold back any complete person-by-person past table pending qualified native-speaker/editor review.
