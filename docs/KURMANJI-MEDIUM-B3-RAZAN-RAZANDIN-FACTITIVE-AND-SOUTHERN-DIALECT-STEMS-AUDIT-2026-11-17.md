# Razan / razandin: factitive meaning, dialectal citation-form homonymy, and stem audit

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only audit. The central finding is that *razandin* has a directly attested transitive factitive use “put to sleep”, but the same-looking citation form also occurs as an intransitive Mardin/Southern Kurmanji infinitive. The two must not be collapsed into one paradigm.

## Executive finding

The dictionaries and dialect grammar establish three related but distinct facts:

1. **Razan** is an intransitive verb “lie down, go to bed, sleep”. Chyet lists present-stem variants **ra-zê-**, **-razê-** (Midyat), and **ra-z-** (Badini). A separate Kurdish verb dictionary gives the example **Nefel raza**.
2. **Razandin** is also a transitive verb “put to sleep, put to bed”, explicitly cross-referenced to **nivandin**. Chyet gives the stem notation **ra-zîn-** and examples **Kevotka Memê dane razandinê** and **Dê zara radizîne**. The Kurdish verb dictionary independently gives **Kewê şîr da kurê xu û razand**.
3. In **Southern Kurmanji (SK), especially the Mardin dialect described by Haig and Öpengin**, **řazandin** is also the intransitive citation form corresponding to Standard Kurmanji **řazan** “sleep”. In that dialectal use, the present stem is **řaz-**, not *řazîn-*, and the past stem is **řaz(ih)a-**, not *řaz(ih)and-*. Their example **Xwe gēr kir řaziha** means “He rolled himself up and slept”.

Therefore, the written form **razandin** alone does not determine transitivity or stem morphology. The dialect and the lexical sense must be identified first.

## 1. Intransitive base: razan

Chyet's *Kurmanji Lexicon* records **ra·zan** as an intransitive verb:
- “to lie down; to go to bed, go to sleep”
- synonymous with **raketin** and **nivistin**
- present-stem notation: **ra-zê-**; Midyat **-razê-**; Badini **ra-z-**
- example: **Divînin bûkek wê xwea razaye** (“They see that the young woman is sleeping”)

The 2007 *Dictionary of Kurdish Verbs* independently lists **Razan** as an intransitive verb meaning **nivistin / to sleep**, with **Nefel raza** as an example.

## 2. Transitive factitive: razandin

Chyet records **ra-zandin** as a transitive verb:
- stem notation: **ra-zîn-**
- sense 1: “to put to sleep, put to bed”, synonym **nivandin**
- example: **Kevotka Memê dane razandinê** (“The doves put Mem to bed”)
- example: **Dê zara radizîne** (“The mother puts the children to sleep”)
- sense 2 in the JJ source: “to spread something out (on the ground)”; this is a separate recorded sense and should not be silently replaced by the sleep meaning.

The 2007 verb dictionary independently records **Razandin** as transitive, “to put to sleep”, and gives **Kewê şîr da kurê xu û razand** (“The partridge gave milk to her son and put him to sleep”). It also records **razandin** with a distinct second sense “to adorn”; context is essential.

These independent sources support teaching the transitive factitive **razandin** with the meaning “خەواندن” and distinguish it from intransitive **razan**.

## 3. Stem evidence for the transitive verb

| Lexeme/use | Present stem | Past evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **razan**, intransitive | **ra-zê-**; variants **-razê-**, **ra-z-** | **raza** directly attested | Present variants and example directly recorded |
| **razandin**, transitive “put to sleep” | **ra-zîn-** | **razand** directly attested | Present stem explicitly given by Chyet; past form directly exemplified |
| **razandin**, Southern Kurmanji intransitive citation form | **raz-** | **raziha-** | Both explicitly analysed by Haig & Öpengin |

Do not derive a complete person/number conjugation table from these isolated forms. The dictionary's stem notation and cited examples are sufficient for the limited contrasts above.

## 4. Critical dialect distinction: Southern Kurmanji / Mardin

Haig and Öpengin explain that Southern Kurmanji (SK) has extended the form **-and(in)** as a general infinitive/citation-form marker. In these cases, the ending does not necessarily carry causative meaning and does not necessarily make the verb transitive.

Their table explicitly aligns:
- Standard Kurmanji **řazan** — “to sleep”
- Southern Kurmanji **řazandin** — “to sleep”

They then state that the intransitive SK verb **řazandin** is not conjugated like a true -and factitive:
- present stem is **řaz-**, not ***řazîn-**
- past stem is **řaz(ih)a-**, not ***řaz(ih)and-**

Their example:
**Xwe gēr kir řaziha.**  
“He rolled himself up (and) slept.”

The paper defines SK here as **Southern Kurmanji**, using data from Midyat and Nusaybin in Mardin province and the wider southern dialect zone. This is not a general claim that every Southern Kurmanji speaker uses every form identically; it is the specific documented dialect pattern in the cited study.

## 5. Teaching-safe contrast

| Form | Meaning | Transitivity | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| **razan** | sleep / lie down | Intransitive | Standard citation form in the comparison |
| **razandin** | put to sleep / put to bed | Transitive | Factitive; stem **ra-zîn-**, past **razand** |
| **řazandin** (SK/Mardin citation form) | sleep | Intransitive | Dialectal infinitive marker; stems **řaz-**, **řaz(ih)a-** |

A learner-facing course should teach the transitive pair **razan → razandin** only with an explicit Standard Kurmanji / dictionary-based label, and should keep the Southern Kurmanji Mardin citation-form pattern in a separate dialect note. Do not teach a universal rule “every -andin is transitive”.

## 6. Kurmanji summary

> **Razan** کردارێکی ناتێپەڕە بە واتای خەوتن و پاڵکەوتن. فەرهەنگی Chyet ڕەگی ئێستای بە شێوەکانی **ra-zê-**، **-razê-** و **ra-z-** تۆمار دەکات؛ فۆڕمی **raza**یش لە نموونەدا هاتووە.  
>
> **Razandin** لە بەکارهێنانی هۆکارسازیی تێپەڕدا بە واتای خەواندنە؛ ڕەگی ئێستای **ra-zîn-** و فۆڕمی ڕابردووی **razand** بە بەڵگە پشتڕاست کراونەتەوە.  
>
> بەڵام لە کرمانجیی باشووری، لە شێوەزاری ماردین، **řazandin** هەروەها دەتوانێت تەنها فۆڕمی بنەڕەتیی کردارێکی ناتێپەڕ بێت بە واتای خەوتن. لەو بەکارهێنانەدا ڕەگی ئێستا **řaz-** و ڕەگی ڕابردوو **řaz(ih)a-** ـە؛ نەک **řazîn-** و **řazand-**. بۆیە تەنها کۆتایی **-andin** بەڵگەی تێپەڕبوونی کردار نییە.
