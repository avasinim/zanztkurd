## 1. Method and evidence labels

This register decomposes the existing source review for Course 1 lessons 19–24 into individual claims. It does not certify every sentence or Sorani translation.

- **SUPPORTED (scope-limited):** the cited source directly supports the grammatical generalization.
- **PARTLY SUPPORTED:** the general pattern is documented, but the exact example, gloss, dialect, or construction needs checking.
- **UNRESOLVED:** the accessible evidence does not justify a firm decision.
- **EDITORIAL:** a sequencing, terminology, assessment, or presentation decision rather than a linguistic fact.

A general grammar does not automatically validate every course example. All exact forms flagged below require review against the project's selected Kurmanji reference variety. Sorani glosses require a separate Sorani editor. No proposed change is authorized by this register.

## 4. Cross-lesson blockers and dependencies

1. **House variety and orthography:** choose the written standard and the spoken reference variety before finalizing forms such as `kengî/kengê`, `li ku/kû/kuderê`, feminine indefinite ezafe and other variants.
2. **Pronoun/case dependency:** lesson 19 needs the `kî/kê` contrast; lesson 23 needs direct/oblique demonstratives; lesson 24 needs oblique possessors. These should use one consistent reviewed paradigm.
3. **Ezafe dependency:** lessons 21 and 24 must distinguish bound linking ezafe from free/demonstrative ezafe and distinguish ezafe agreement from case.
4. **Comparison dependency:** lesson 22 needs explicit distinction between regular `-tir`, `herî`, `-tirîn`, and lexical/variant forms.
5. **Syntax dependency:** lesson 20 must preserve neutral word order, focus/fronting, and past-transitive alignment as separate topics.
6. **Example provenance:** every sentence should be tagged internally as directly sourced, adapted, or instructor-authored and independently checked. Do not imply the general grammar source directly attests a course sentence unless it does.
7. **Sorani editorial gate:** every Sorani gloss and explanation needs independent language review.
8. **Pathway B gate:** Kurmanji-only learner material must be independently authored and reviewed; it is not a literal translation of Pathway A.
