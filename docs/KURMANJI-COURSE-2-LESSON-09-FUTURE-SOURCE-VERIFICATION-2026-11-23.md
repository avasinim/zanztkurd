# Course 2 Lesson 09 — Future tense source verification
Date: 2026-11-23
Scope: verify the existing lesson's future-tense claim and one example against Haig (2018), §2.6.4. Documentation-only.

## Finding
Haig (2018), §2.6.4, explicitly states that the future is expressed with particle (d)ê or wê combined with a present subjunctive form. The particle is commonly clause-second. High-frequency verbs such as gotin and bûn generally drop the bi- prefix in future contexts. The section also gives multiple examples.

## Direct source examples (retain source orthography)
- (27) `ew dē mesel-ē ji te re bēž-e` — glossed “He will tell you the issue.”
- (28) `wē čawa heval-ēn nexweş derbas bi-bi-n` — “How will the wounded friends pass?”
- (29) `ewro ne, dē sibe či-m` — “not today, (but) tomorrow I will come.”

Example (27) specifically illustrates dē in clause-second position with `bēž-e`, and not a bi- prefix on this high-frequency verb. Example (29) uses `či-m` without bi-.
