# xwendin past-conditional negation and paradigm evidence audit (2026-11-17)

## Findings from Kurmanji-specific sources

### 1. Thackston: negative past conditional
W. M. Thackston, *Kurmanji Kurdish: A Reference Grammar with Selected Readings*, §25.1, gives the affirmative transitive paradigm with `bí-` and the negative paradigm with **`ne-`**, e.g.:
- `wî bidîtama`
- `wî bidîtayî`
- `wî bidîta`
- `wî bidîtana`
- `wî nedîtama`
- `wî nedîtana`

The same section explicitly says past conditionals of transitive verbs are ergative. Full Kurmanji PDF:
https://www.kurdipedia.org/files/books/2013/92279.PDF
Relevant extracted lines in the PDF: §25.1 around 1990–2003; prose example `wî ê bixwenda` around 2125–2130.

### 2. Songül Gündoğdu: negation blocks the subjunctive prefix
Gündoğdu, “Negation in Kurmanji,” in *Affix Ordering Across Languages and Frameworks* (Oxford University Press, 2015), chapter 7:
https://www.academia.edu/3342645/Negation_in_Kurmanji_In_Manova_ed_Affix_Ordering_Across_Languages_and_Frameworks_Oxford_University_Press
The chapter's data are elicited from native speakers of Kurmanji in Muş and also include Thackston and Bedirxan & Lescot. It states that negation and TAM prefixes occupy the same prefix slot and cannot co-occur. Its Table 7.8 directly gives affirmative and negative paradigms for `çûn`, including `biçûma` vs `neçûma`, `biçûya` vs `neçûya`, `biçûna` vs `neçûna`. It further analyzes the conditional as circumfixation of `bi-` plus past stem plus agreement and conditional suffix `-(y)a`; negation blocks the `bi-` prefix.

### 3. Exact xwendin form
Thackston §25.1 prose example on printed p. 65 directly attests `wî ê bixwenda` (“he would study”). This confirms `bixwenda` in context, but does not provide a complete `xwendin` paradigm.

## Evidence status for projected xwendin forms
Given the attested past stem `xwend-`, the consonant-final pattern, and the explicit negative-prefix alternation, the following forms can be generated by rule:

| Object agreement | Affirmative projection | Negative projection |
|---|---|---|
| 1sg | `wî bixwendama` | `wî nexwendama` |
| 2sg | `wî bixwendayî` | `wî nexwendayî` |
| 3sg | `wî bixwenda` | `wî nexwenda` |
| plural | `wî bixwendana` | `wî nexwendana` |

Important distinctions:
- `wî ê bixwenda` is a directly attested contextual phrase; the bare affirmative verb form `bixwenda` is present within it.
- The negative `nexwenda` and the other negative `xwendin` person/object forms are **rule-based projections**, not directly quoted `xwendin` examples from Thackston or Gündoğdu.
- The remaining affirmative forms are also projections from the paradigm, except the directly attested third-person form `bixwenda`.
- The oblique agent `wî` is held constant here only to illustrate the transitive ergative pattern; actual agent choice and object case/person must fit the intended sentence.
- Do not conflate these past conditional forms with past perfect conditional forms or the future-modal particle `ê`.

## Dialect and policy caveats
Thackston describes a broad written Kurmanji norm and explicitly notes regional variation. Gündoğdu's empirical data are from Muş Kurmanji. Their evidence should not be merged into a claim that every dialect has identical surface forms. The Academy's selected written/spoken variety remains a separate approval decision.

## Recommendation
For the Academy research ledger:
1. Record `bixwenda` as directly attested.
2. Record `ne-` replacing `bi-` in negative past conditionals as a directly supported general Kurmanji pattern.
3. Mark the full `xwendin` affirmative and negative paradigms as generated projections pending direct corpus or paradigm attestation and Academy review.
4. Do not edit learner-facing lessons or declare the paradigm Academy-standard.
