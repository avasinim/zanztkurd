# Dengnasî source audit — Lesson 23 (2026-11-26)

## Scope

Lesson 23, **Cudahiyên dengnasî di navbera kurmancî û soranî de**, is based on Husein Muhammed, *Dengnasî di kurdî de*, printed pages 373–386 (PDF physical pages 398–411).

## Source-backed details restored

A targeted comparison found several source footnotes/examples that were not represented in the lesson text. They have now been restored in a dedicated source-notes section and synchronized to the standalone HTML preview and JSON payload:

1. The rare native Sorani exception **hevde** to the V/W correspondence (source footnote 276).
2. The note that Sorani **kat** is more commonly used for “time/period” although **dem** also exists with that meaning (footnote 277).
3. Alternative verb forms **gehandin / gehiştin** (footnote 280).
4. The **t-** present-continuous prefix exceptions, including **ez têm / tînim** (footnote 281).
5. The dialect distribution of Sorani **de-** (Mukri and Hewlêrî) and **e-** (Silemanî and Kerkûkî), with **de-** more prevalent in written Sorani (footnote 282).
6. The inflected form **kê** for **kî** (footnote 283).
7. The Italian C examples **cento** [çênto] and **cinque** [çînkwê], illustrating C before E/I.
8. The gloss for **dijûn**: “sixêr, xeber”.
9. The source's cross-reference for the historical discussion of H, retained as its cited URL.

No new phonological rule or unrelated example was introduced. These are source notes, not additional exercises or quizzes.

## Source ambiguity preserved

The source's G/K section states that K before Ê/WÊ may be pronounced [ç], but its example **kî, kê → kê** is transcribed [kê], not [çê]. The lesson continues to flag this as an internal source inconsistency and does not silently normalize it.
