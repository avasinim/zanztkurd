# Dengnasi source audit — Lesson 10

Date: 2026-11-26  
Source: Husein Muhammed, *Dengnasî di kurdî de*, printed pp. 116–132 (designed PDF pp. 129–145).  
Status: second-pass source reconciliation; draft branch only.

## Reviewed coverage

- H / Ḧ distinction, IPA [h] and [ħ], and standard H spelling versus optional scholarly ḧ notation.
- Arabic-origin and native [ħ] vocabulary; eastern/central Kurmanji distribution; H variants and borrowing paths.
- H articulation, distribution, frequency and Sorani word-final comparison.
- Historical H sources and changes: PIE S before vowels, intervocalic D, prothetic H, H correspondences to X/W/Ş, and H/W/Sorani outcomes.
- J articulation, spelling across scripts, distribution/frequency, historical correspondences, devoicing before T, dialectal shortening, European loanword variants, and final J loss.

## Source-backed details restored in this pass

1. Expanded *ḧinêr* gloss to include *pêçêbûn* and *jêhatin* alongside strength and ability.
2. Corrected the semantic description of *ḧal*: market/bazaar, especially a covered market; retained the French *halle*, German *Halle*, English *hall* connections and the possible influence of Arabic *ḧal* (“condition/state”).
3. Added the source's *ḧesin* / *asin* and *çehv* / *çav* comparisons, and distinguished Kurdish S < PIE *ḱ in *ser*, *spî*, *sed* from the separate S → H development.
4. Restored the *-rêj-* + T outcomes *rişt* and *rêt*, and the source's explicit rejection of *rijt*.

## Deliberate exclusions / constraints

- Source footnote 90 points to external audio recordings. These are intentionally not reproduced because this course is text-only and must not depend on audio.
- Arabic-script source spellings and scripts from other alphabets are not copied into the Latin-only lesson.
- No quizzes, questions, tests, exercises, answer keys or audio were added.
