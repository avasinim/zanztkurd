# xwendin past-conditional direct negative attestation (2026-11-17)

## New direct source evidence

A direct negative example of the Kurmanji transitive past conditional with **xwendin** has now been located in two independent grammar/textbook sources.

### 1. Murad Ciwan, *Kürtçe Dilbilgisi: Kurmanç Lehçesi* (Jîna Nû, 1992), p. 124

The source labels the construction as the past conditional ("sanal geçmiş zaman") and gives the affirmative and negative transitive paradigms. The negative table explicitly includes:

- `Min ê nexwenda`
- `Te ê nexwenda`
- `Wî/Wê (y)ê nexwenda`
- `Me ê nexwenda`
- `We yê nexwenda`
- `Wan ê nexwenda`

The affirmative table on the same page includes `Min ê bixwenda`, `Te ê bixwenda`, `Wî/Wê (y)ê bixwenda`, `Me ê bixwenda`, `We yê bixwenda`, and `Wan ê bixwenda`.

### 2. Têrêza Amryan & Vardan Voskanyan, *Pirtûka Dersa Kurdî (Kurmancî)*, Volume II (Yerevan State University Press, 2025)

A separate recent university textbook dialogue directly includes:
- `Ger mamoste bigota, teyê bixwenda?`
- `Na, minê nexwenda.`

This is a direct negative xwendin example in context, with the affirmative question immediately preceding it.

## Updated evidence classification

- `bixwenda`: directly attested affirmative xwendin past conditional.
- `nexwenda`: directly attested negative xwendin past conditional, independently supported by the 1992 grammar paradigm and 2025 Yerevan State University textbook dialogue.
- The full affirmative and negative transitive paradigm with `Min/Te/Wî-Wê/Me/We/Wan` is directly tabulated by Ciwan (1992), p. 124.
- These sources directly establish the construction and forms in their respective descriptions/registers. They do not, by themselves, establish that every regional spoken variety uses identical forms or that the Academy has approved one standard.

## Important distinction: construction and temporal interpretation

Ciwan labels the paradigm as a past conditional. The Yerevan textbook dialogue frames it with `Ger ... bigota` and gives `minê nexwenda` as the response. Do not collapse this construction into the ordinary past perfect (`nexwendibû`) or the ordinary future (`nexwînim`).

## Research status update

The earlier audit correctly marked the complete xwendin paradigm as projected because it lacked exact direct negative xwendin citations at that time. This new evidence supersedes that provisional status for `nexwenda` and for the tabulated paradigm in Ciwan.
