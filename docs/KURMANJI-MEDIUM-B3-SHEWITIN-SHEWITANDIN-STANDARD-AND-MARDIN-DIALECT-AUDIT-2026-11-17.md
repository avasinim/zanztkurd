# Şewitîn / şewitandin: Standard Kurmanji factitive and Mardin citation-form variation

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only clarification of the Standard Kurmanji intransitive/transitive pair and a separately documented Mardin dialect pattern. This note supplements, rather than replaces, the earlier *mirin / mirandin / şewitîn / şewitandin* audit.

## Executive finding

The pair is a well-supported factitive contrast in Standard Kurmanji:

- **şewitîn** — “to burn, be on fire”; intransitive.
- **şewitandin** — “to cause to burn, set fire to, burn something”; transitive.
- Standard Kurmanji present stem of the factitive: **şewitîn-**.
- The Thackston example directly contrasts **Mala wî şewita** (“His house burned down”) with **Wan mala wî şewitand** (“They burned his house down”).

However, Haig and Öpengin document a distinct Mardin dialect citation-form pattern: Standard Kurmanji **şewitîn** corresponds to Mardin **şewit’andin**, glossed “to burn (intr.)”. In this variety, the ending resembling the Standard Kurmanji factitive **-andin** can occur in an intransitive infinitive without adding causative meaning. It must not be treated as the ordinary Standard Kurmanji transitive factitive merely because its spelling resembles it.

## 1. Standard Kurmanji: direct factitive evidence

Thackston, §28, explicitly defines factitive verbs as forms that render an intransitive base transitive. His table gives:

- base infinitive: **şewitan** (“to burn”)
- base present stem: **şewit-**
- factitive infinitive: **şewitandin**
- factitive present stem: **şewitîn-**
- factitive meaning: “to make burn, set fire to”

The paired examples are:

- **Mala wî şewita.** — His house burned down.
- **Wan mala wî şewitand.** — They burned his house down.

Haig and Öpengin use the modern Standard Kurmanji citation form **şewitîn** for the intransitive verb and **şewitandin** for its factitive. The apparent difference between Thackston's **şewitan** and Haig and Öpengin's **şewitîn** is recorded here as a source/orthographic citation-form difference; it should not be silently normalized without a project orthography decision.

## 2. Mardin dialect: -andin does not always mean factitive

Haig and Öpengin explain that in Mardin (their “SK” variety), the suffix **-and** has been extended as a general infinitive/citation-form marker for many verbs. It can occur with intransitive verbs without transitivizing them.

Their comparison table explicitly lists:

| Standard Kurmanji | Mardin form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **şewitîn** | **şewit’andin** | “to burn (intr.)” |

The authors further state that such intransitive Mardin forms are not inflected according to the regular paradigm of Standard Kurmanji factitives derived with **-and**. Their spelling therefore cannot be used to infer a transitive meaning, a factitive present stem, or a factitive past stem.

The Mardin form is a dialect-specific citation-form pattern, not a replacement for the Standard Kurmanji pair.

## 3. Stem and agreement boundaries

Safe Standard Kurmanji facts:
- Intransitive example: **Mala wî şewita.**
- Transitive factitive example: **Wan mala wî şewitand.**
- Factitive present stem: **şewitîn-**.
- The transitive past example shows the agent in the oblique form **wan** and the verb form **şewitand** with a third-person singular patient (“his house”).

Not established by these citations:
- a complete past-tense object-agreement paradigm for *şewitandin*;
- a complete set of present forms or imperative forms;
- the full inflectional paradigm of Mardin **şewit’andin**.

Do not manufacture these forms from the spelling of the infinitive. The ordinary Kurmanji past-tense pattern involves object agreement in transitive clauses, but that general rule does not supply the missing verb-specific paradigm.

## 4. Teaching-safe conclusion

> لە کرمانجیی ستاندارددا **şewitîn** بە واتای سووتان کردارێکی ناتێپەڕە؛ **şewitandin** بە واتای سووتاندنی شتێک کردارێکی تێپەڕ و هۆکارسازییە. بەڵام لە شێوەی ماردین، **şewit’andin** هەمان واتای ناتێپەڕی «سووتان» هەیە؛ بۆیە تەنها لەسەر بنەمای کۆتایی **-andin** نابێت واتا و ڕێزمانی ستانداردی لێوەربگیرێت.
