# Xwendin / xwandin: orthographic variants, present and past stems audit

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only lexical and morphological audit for the Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway.

## Executive finding

The verb means **“to read; to study”**. Sources represent its infinitive as both **xwendin** and **xwandin**. The consulted material supports treating these as orthographic/source variants of the same lexical verb for this audit, not as two different meanings.

The stem contrast is stable across the consulted descriptions:
- Present stem: **xwîn-**
- Past stem: **xwend-** when the infinitive is written **xwendin**; **xwand-** in sources that write **xwandin**.
- Present 1sg: **Ez dixwînim.**
- Past transitive example: **Min xwend.** (The form is also represented as **xwand** in the source spelling that uses *xwandin*.)

Do not form the present stem by simply carrying the infinitive's **-wend- / -wand-** sequence into the present. The present stem is **xwîn-**, not *xwend-* or *xwand-*.

## 1. Lexical identity and spelling

Haig & Öpengin's Northern Kurdish grammar lists **xwandin** with the meaning “read, study”. A Kurmanji teaching text and the *Rêzimana Kurdî ya Kurmancî* source use **xwendin** for the same meaning. The latter explicitly records both infinitive spellings and pairs them with their corresponding past stems:
- **xwendin** → **xwend-**
- **xwandin** → **xwand-**

This supports a transparent source-spelling correspondence. It does not by itself establish which spelling the Academy must standardize. Until the Academy's orthography policy is approved, keep both forms documented and label each citation by its source spelling. Do not silently normalize quotations.

## 2. Present stem

The sources consistently identify the present stem as **xwîn-**:
- Haig & Öpengin: **xwandin**, past **xwand-**, present **-xwîn-**.
- Kurmanji beginner grammar: **xwendin**, present **xwîn-**.
- *Rêzimana Kurdî ya Kurmancî*: **xwendin (xwandin)** and **-xwîn-** as present/imperative stem.

The present indicative is formed with **di- + xwîn- + personal ending**:
- **Ez dixwînim.** — I read / I study.
- **Tu dixwînî.**
- **Ew dixwîne.**
- **Em dixwînin.**

These examples illustrate the present stem. The Academy should apply its approved orthographic policy consistently to circumflexes and vowel representation.

## 3. Past stem and transitivity

The past stem is source-spelling dependent:
- With **xwendin**, write **xwend-**.
- With **xwandin**, write **xwand-**.

The verb is transitive in the relevant “read/study something” construction. The Kurmanji learning material illustrates the past with **Min xwend** (“I read”), where **min** is the oblique past agent and the verb form is **xwend** when the object is third-person singular/understood from context. Do not infer a full agreement paradigm from this single example.

## 4. Distinction from xwarin and vexwarin

| Verb | Meaning | Present stem | Past stem |
|---|---|---|---|
| **xwarin** | eat | **xw-** | **xwar-** |
| **vexwarin** | drink | **xw-**, with preverb **ve-** | **xwar-**, with **ve-** |
| **xwendin / xwandin** | read / study | **xwîn-** | **xwend- / xwand-** |

The shared initial letters do not imply shared lexical identity or identical stem formation. In particular:
- **xwarin**: **Ez dixwim** (“I eat”).
- **vexwarin**: **Ez vedixwim** (“I drink”).
- **xwendin**: **Ez dixwînim** (“I read / study”).

Keep these as separate dictionary and teaching entries.

## 5. Teaching-safe decision

For the Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway:
1. Teach one lexical entry meaning “read / study”.
2. Record **xwendin** and **xwandin** as attested source spellings; do not present them as separate verbs unless later dialect evidence justifies that distinction.
3. Teach present stem **xwîn-** and the form **Ez dixwînim**.
4. Teach past stem according to the chosen spelling: **xwend-** with *xwendin*, **xwand-** with *xwandin*.
5. Keep **xwarin** (“eat”) and **vexwarin** (“drink”) in separate entries.
6. Defer a single Academy-wide spelling choice until the written-standard policy is explicitly approved.
