# Kurmancî Medium B3 — ragihandin and sipartin: prefixation, stems, transitivity, and past evidence audit

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only lexical and grammatical audit. No lesson text, course inventory, runtime, CI, seed, database, migration, PR, or deployment changes.

## Executive findings

| Infinitive | Meaning | Transitivity | Present stem | Past stem | 1sg present | Attested past |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ragihandin | announce, report, communicate, make known | transitive | gihîn- with preverb ra- | ragihand- | radigihînim | ragihand (3sg patient); full lexical paradigm listed |
| sipartin | entrust, hand over, consign, delegate | transitive | spêr- | sipart- | ez disipêrim | min tu sipartî (2sg patient) |

The two verbs must not be treated as members of one identical stem-formation class. **ragihandin** is transparently related to gihandin with the preverb **ra-**; **sipartin** has its own listed present stem **spêr-**, not a mechanically predictable *sipar-* or *sipêr-* inferred from spelling alone.

## 1. ragihandin

### Meaning, stems, and present paradigm

The Kurmancî Wiktionary-derived Kaikki entry labels ragihandin a transitive Kurmancî verb and records the senses: announce, proclaim, report, communicate, disseminate, make known, advertise, notify. It lists:
- 1sg present: **radigihînim**
- 2sg present: **radigihînî**
- 3sg present: **radigihîne**
- plural present: **radigihînin**
- singular imperative: **ragihîne**
- plural imperative: **ragihînin**
- simple-past citation form: **ragihand**

The lexical analysis is **ra- + gihandin**. The base verb gihandin has present stem **gihîn-** and past stem **gihand-**; ragihandin retains these stems with the preverb ra-. Thus the present form is built as **ra-di-gihîn-im** and the simple past as **ra-gihand**. This segmentation is an analysis of the attested forms, not a claim that every preverb behaves identically in every tense or dialect.

### Past and agreement

The Kaikki lexical entry lists ragihand for all six oblique-agent citation rows, representing the third-person-singular patient shape. The general Kurmancî transitive-past rule is oblique agent + direct patient, with the verb agreeing with the patient. However, no verb-specific non-3sg patient form for ragihandin was independently verified in the sources consulted. Do not invent a full patient-agreement table.

No directly inspected literary simple-past quotation for ragihandin was verified in this audit. The lexical paradigm is useful evidence, but it is not a substitute for a literary attestation.

### Contrast

- **gihandin**: deliver/convey/bring something to someone or somewhere.
- **ragihandin**: announce, communicate, make known, report.
- **gihiştin**: arrive/reach; intransitive, with a distinct stem set.

Do not confuse the semantic relation between gihandin and ragihandin with the transitivity contrast between gihandin and gihiştin.

## 2. sipartin

### Meaning and stems

Kurmanci.app's Faik Şahin dictionary entry labels **sipartin** transitive and cross-references **spartin**, with the senses “entrust, hand over, consign, refer/delegate”. The same dictionary entry for spartin records the present-stem notation **-spêr- / -sipêr-** and gives sipartin as an alternative form.

The Ich lerne Kurmancî verb list gives the explicit paradigm:
- infinitive: **sipartin**
- present stem: **spêr-**
- 1sg present: **ez disipêrim**
- singular imperative: **bisipêre**
- past stem/form: **sipart**

This supports the core stem contrast:
- present: **spêr-**
- past: **sipart-**

Do not infer the present stem from the written infinitive by a general suffix rule; memorize the lexical stem pair.

### Past example and object agreement

Faik Şahin's dictionary gives:
**Min tu sipartî Xwedê û min berê xwe da riya xerîbiyê.**

The first clause means “I entrusted you to God”; it contains oblique agent **min**, direct patient **tu**, and verb **sipartî**. This is a verb-specific 2sg patient example and directly supports the object-agreement form **sipartî**.

The same dictionary gives another plural-patient clause:
**Bavê nexweş mal û milkê xwe spartin xêrxwazan û zarokên xwe parastin.**
The predicate **spartin** appears with a coordinated/plural patient phrase “mal û milkê xwe” (property and possessions). The clause is useful corroborating evidence for plural agreement, although the coordination and lexical variant should be preserved as printed rather than silently normalized to sipartin.

### sipartin / spartin / sipêr- / spêr-

Sources record several related forms:
- sipartin: lemma/variant spelling
- spartin: cross-referenced form in Faik Şahin
- spêr-: present stem in the beginner verb list
- sipêr-: variant present-stem notation in Faik Şahin's dictionary
- sipart-: past stem

A Kurmanji-English lexicon records **sipartin sipêr- = spartin**, and the Chyet/Thackston reference-grammar lexical material records **sipartin sipr- = spartin** in its OCR-transcribed vocabulary. This variation is a warning against treating the vowel sequence in the infinitive as a single invariant orthographic norm. Preserve source-specific spelling and stem notation until a qualified Standard Kurmancî editor confirms the project's preferred headword and variant policy.

### Secondary sense

The dictionary also records **xwe spartin dîwêr** (“to lean oneself against the wall”). This is a distinct reflexive construction/extended use and should not be mixed into the core “entrust/hand over” transitive example without explaining the construction.

## 3. Alignment, negation, and aspect guardrails

The academic overview by Haig & Öpengin describes the Kurmancî split alignment: ordinary transitive past clauses use an oblique agent and an absolutive/direct patient, with verb agreement tracking the patient. The source also notes dialectal erosion/restructuring in some spoken subvarieties. Therefore the documented example **Min tu sipartî** is a useful Standard Kurmancî pattern, but it should not be presented as universal across all local speech varieties.

No verb-specific negative paradigms were directly verified for either verb here. Do not create negative examples and label them as quotations or attested forms. Likewise, do not conflate simple past forms with participles or compound perfect constructions.

## 4. Evidence grading and teaching recommendation

- **High confidence:** ragihandin is transitive; the present forms radigihînim/radigihînî/radigihîne/radigihînin and past citation form ragihand are listed by the Kurmancî lexical entry.
- **High confidence:** ragihandin is morphologically related to gihandin through preverb ra-; the base stem pair gihîn-/gihand- is independently listed.
- **High confidence:** sipartin is transitive; the beginner verb list explicitly gives spêr-, ez disipêrim, bisipêre, sipart.
- **High confidence:** Faik Şahin gives a direct 2sg-patient past example, **Min tu sipartî Xwedê**, and a plural coordinated-patient example with **spartin**.
- **Unresolved editorial issue:** sipartin/spartin and spêr-/sipêr-/sipr- variation needs a declared orthographic/lexicographic policy before course standardization.
- **Not established here:** full person-by-person past agreement paradigm for ragihandin; verb-specific negative paradigms; a directly page-verified literary past quotation for ragihandin.
- **Teaching recommendation:** teach ragihandin as a preverb-derived transitive verb and contrast its meaning with gihandin. Teach sipartin with the sourced 2sg past example, but annotate the spelling/stem variants and request native-speaker/editor review before fixing a single headword.
