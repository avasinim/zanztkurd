# Vexwarin: compound structure, present/past stems, and distinction from xwarin/xwendin

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only lexical and morphological audit for the Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway.

## Executive finding

**vexwarin** is the ordinary Northern Kurmanji verb “to drink”. It is a compound verb built from the preverbal particle **ve-** plus **xwarin** “to eat”. It is not a derived factitive in **-andin**.

The most useful directly supported analysis is:
- Infinitive: **vexwarin**
- Structure: **ve- + xwarin**
- Present stem of the lexical verb: **xw-**, with the preverbal particle **ve-** retained outside the finite verb complex.
- Present 1sg: **vedixwim** (also pedagogically segmented **ve-di-xw-im**).
- Past: **vexwar** is directly attested in a Northern Kurdish valency example; the underlying past stem is **xwar-**, inherited from **xwarin**.
- Transitivity: transitive; the thing drunk is the direct object.

Do not confuse **vexwarin** “drink” with **xwarin** “eat”, or with **xwendin / xwandin** “read, study”. The similar consonant sequences do not make these the same verb.

## 1. Lexical meaning and construction

The Kurmanji Wiktionary entry gives the etymology **ve- + xwarin** and the verbal meaning “to drink”. It explicitly gives the present stem as **ve-xw-**. The same page also records **vexwarin** as a feminine noun meaning “a drink” or “the act of drinking”; grammatical category must therefore be read from sentence context.

The *Ferhengên Kurmancî* entry lists:
- **vexwarin** as a noun: a beverage / drink;
- **vexwarin / ve dixwe / vexw e** as a verb, with senses including drinking and consuming/swallowing.

These lexicographic entries support both the verb and noun uses. They do not license treating the noun and verb as one grammatical category.

## 2. Present-tense morphology

The Kurmanji beginner grammar explicitly describes **vexwarin** as a compound verb consisting of particle **ve-** plus **xwarin**. It states that the present-tense marker **di-** comes between the preverbal particle and the present stem:
- **ve-di-xw-im** → **vedixwim** (“I drink”).

The same source gives the present forms:
- **ez vedixwim**
- **tu vedixwî**
- **ew vedixwe**
- **em vedixwin**
- **hûn vedixwin**
- **ew vedixwin**

The form **vedixwim** is independently supported by a Kurmanji learning resource and by a beginner textbook exercise set.

## 3. Past-tense evidence

The BivalTyp Northern Kurdish valency database gives a transitive example:
- **zilam şîr vexwar**
- “the man drank the milk”

This directly attests **vexwar** as a 3sg perfective/past form and confirms the direct-object construction. Since the base **xwarin** has past stem **xwar-** in Haig & Öpengin's stem table, the compound past is transparently **ve- + xwar-** in the cited example.

Do not extrapolate a complete past-tense paradigm from this one attested example.

## 4. Transitivity and argument structure

BivalTyp classifies **vexwarin** as transitive (TR), with:
- X: transitive subject;
- Y: direct object.

Its example **zilam şîr vexwar** (“the man drank the milk”) demonstrates the object directly. This is a useful teaching example because it distinguishes **vexwarin** from an intransitive verb and provides an attested past form.

## 5. Distinction from xwarin and xwendin

| Verb | Meaning | Present stem | Past stem | Important note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **xwarin** | eat | **xw-** | **xwar-** | Simple transitive verb |
| **vexwarin** | drink | **xw-** with **ve-** particle | **xwar-** with **ve-** | Compound verb; **di-** appears after **ve-** in present |
| **xwendin** (also source spelling **xwandin**) | read / study | **xwîn-** in Haig & Öpengin | **xwend- / xwand-** depending on source/orthographic representation | Separate lexical verb; do not merge with **xwarin** |

Haig & Öpengin list **xwarin** (“eat”) with past **xwar-**, present **-xw-**, and **xwandin** (“read, study”) with past **xwand-**, present **-xwîn-**. The beginner textbook and Kurmanji reference sources also distinguish the two meanings. Orthographic variation between **xwendin** and **xwandin** should be recorded according to the project's adopted standard rather than silently mixed.

## 6. Teaching-safe decision

For the Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway:
1. Teach **xwarin** = “eat” and **vexwarin** = “drink” as separate lexical verbs.
2. Explain **vexwarin** as **ve- + xwarin**, a compound/preverb construction, not a factitive **-andin** derivative.
3. Teach the present pattern **ve-di-xw-im → vedixwim**, where **di-** occurs between **ve-** and **xw-**.
4. Use **zilam şîr vexwar** as a directly attested transitive past example.
5. Keep **xwendin / xwandin** (“read, study”) in a separate entry, with a clear note about source spelling.
6. If teaching the noun **vexwarin** (“a drink”), label it explicitly as a noun.
