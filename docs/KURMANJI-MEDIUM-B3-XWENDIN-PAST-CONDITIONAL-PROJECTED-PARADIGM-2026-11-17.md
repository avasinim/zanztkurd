# xwendin past-conditional paradigm: source-grounded derivation, not attestation (2026-11-17)

## Purpose
Record what can and cannot responsibly be concluded about the Kurmanji past conditional of `xwendin`, using the verified Kurmanji edition of W. M. Thackston rather than the separate Sorani grammar.

## Verified source and rule
W. M. Thackston, *Kurmanji Kurdish: A Reference Grammar with Selected Readings*, §25.1 (printed p. 61; Scribd text extraction around lines 2318–2340):
- Forms the past conditional by prefixing **bí-** to the past stem and adding the person/object endings.
- For consonant-final stems, the endings shown are **-ama, -ayî, -a** for singular absolutive objects and **-ana** for plural absolutive objects.
- Explicitly states that past conditionals of intransitives are non-ergative, while those of transitives are ergative.
- Gives the transitive paradigm with a constant oblique agent **wî** and changing object agreement: `wî bidîtama`, `wî bidîtayî`, `wî bidîta`, `wî bidîtana`; negative forms include `wî nedîtama`.

Source page (full title, author, contents, preface, and §25.1 visible):
https://de.scribd.com/document/369311993/Kurmanji-Kurdish-Grammar-pdf
Relevant source sections: lines 42–48 (title/author), 105–108 (contents), 160–171 (Kurmanji/Sorani distinction), 2318–2340 (§25.1).

## Morphological projection for xwendin (NOT directly attested in the cited paradigm)
Because `xwend` ends in a consonant, mechanically applying Thackston's consonant-final-stem pattern to this past stem yields the following **analytical projections**:

| Absolutive object | Projected form with constant agent `wî` |
|---|---|
| 1st person singular | `wî bixwendama` |
| 2nd person singular | `wî bixwendayî` |
| 3rd person singular | `wî bixwenda` |
| plural object | `wî bixwendana` |

Negative projections by analogy with Thackston's `wî nedîtama` pattern would be `wî nexwendama`, `wî nexwendayî`, `wî nexwenda`, `wî nexwendana`.

**Critical status:** these `xwendin` forms are generated by combining (a) the attested past stem `xwend` and (b) the general consonant-final past-conditional pattern exemplified with `dîtin`. They are not examples printed in Thackston, and no independent source found in this audit directly attests this full `xwendin` paradigm. They must not be presented to learners as source-verified forms or as Academy-approved standard forms.

## Dialect and orthography caveats
- Thackston describes a broad written Kurmanji norm and notes regional variation in the preface. The Academy has not yet selected/approved a specific dialect implementation for this form.
- Keep Thackston's two past-perfect conditional variants in §25.2 separate; they are described as mutually exclusive according to dialect. This note concerns §25.1 only.
- Do not import the Sorani Thackston PDF or Sorani morphology into this Kurmanji analysis.
- No pronunciation/audio scoring or learner-facing lesson change is authorized by this audit.

## Decision
The rule is sufficiently documented to record a **provisional morphological projection** for research and review. The exact `xwendin` conditional paradigm remains **not directly attested**. Keep it out of learner-facing content until corroborated by an independent Kurmanji-specific source or approved by the Academy's language reviewer.
