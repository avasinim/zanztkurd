# Xewtin / xewandin: dialect attribution, source language, and limits of the factitive analysis

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only audit for the Kurmanji-medium pathway. The key issue is dialect attribution: the clearest published xewtin / xewandin pair is explicitly associated with Southern Kurdish / “Lower Kurmanji (Sorani)” in the sources, not established as a Standard Northern Kurmanji pair.

## Executive finding

The available evidence does **not** justify teaching **xewtin → xewandin** as a productive Standard Northern Kurmanji factitive pair.

- Taufiq Wahby and C. J. Edmonds' *A Kurdish-English Dictionary* (1966) records **xewtin (xew)** as an intransitive verb “sleep”. The dictionary explicitly says its standard language is based on the Southern-Kirmanji dialect of Sulaimani in Iraq (its historical terminology for the variety now commonly called Central/Sorani Kurdish), not the Northern Kurmanji/Hawar standard targeted by this pathway.
- Deham Ebdulfettah's article on **-andin** explicitly says **xew + andin = xewandin**, and gives **xewtin** as the related intransitive infinitive. It then explicitly states that, among these noun-derived examples, **xewtin / xewandin** is the pair used in “Kurmanciya Jêrîn (Soranî)” (Lower Kurmanji / Sorani).
- Glosbe lists **xewtin** only as a less frequent Kurmanji translation for “sleep”; its main Kurmanji translations are **razan, nivistin, raketin**. This is not enough to establish a Northern Kurmanji conjugation or a standard transitive factitive **xewandin**.
- Wiktionary's English page for Central Kurdish **خەوتن (xewtin)** explicitly labels it Central Kurdish and gives present stem **xew-**; its Northern Kurdish cross-reference is **xeftin**, not *xewtin*. This is further reason not to transfer the Central/Sorani form directly into Northern Kurmanji.

## 1. Source-language and dialect attribution

### Wahby & Edmonds (1966)

The dictionary introduction (p. vi) identifies its language as the standard literary language based on the **Southern-Kirmanji dialect of Sulaimani** in Iraq. This historical label must not be mistaken for the modern Northern Kurmanji/Hawar standard.

The dictionary's entry at p. 158 records:
- **xewtin (xew)** — intransitive, “sleep”.
- The present stem is explicitly **xew**.
- Under the dictionary's regular verb conventions, the infinitive and stem are supplied; the past form **xewt** is independently echoed in Deham's article as **Ew xewt**.

This is reliable evidence for the Sulaimani/Sorani-side form, not direct evidence for Standard Northern Kurmanji.

### Deham Ebdulfettah (2011)

The article *Paşgira (Andin) Di Zimanê Kurdî De* describes several types of -andin formation. It first gives common intransitive-to-transitive pairs such as **razan → razandin**, **mirin → mirandin**, and **şewitîn → şewitandin**.

In a separate section, it discusses noun-derived formations:
- **xew + andin = xewandin**
- related infinitive **xewtin**
- example for the intransitive verb: **Ew xewt** (“he/she slept”).

Crucially, the author says that, in practical use, among the noun-derived examples in that section, **xewtin / xewandin** is the one used in **Kurmanciya Jêrîn (Soranî)**. This statement locates the pair in the Lower Kurmanji/Sorani usage described by the author. It does not certify it as Standard Northern Kurmanji.

The article's equation of a noun base **xew** plus **-andin** is also structurally distinct from the productive factitive rule that derives a transitive verb from the present stem of a Northern Kurmanji intransitive verb. Do not automatically apply the Northern Kurmanji present-stem rule for **-andin** to this noun-derived, dialect-marked item.

## 2. What can be said about stems?

| Form | Meaning | Evidence | Safe conclusion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **xewtin** | sleep | Wahby & Edmonds: intransitive, present stem **xew**; Deham: **Ew xewt** | Confirmed for the Sulaimani/Sorani-side source variety |
| **xewt-** | past stem | Deham's example **Ew xewt** | Attested example; do not infer a complete paradigm |
| **xewandin** | put to sleep / causative of sleep | Deham identifies the noun-derived pair **xew + andin** and says the pair is used in Lower Kurmanji/Sorani | Dialect-attributed lexical/derivational evidence; not a Standard Northern Kurmanji teaching item |
| **xewîn-**, **xewand-** | proposed stems | No reliable direct paradigm found in the consulted sources | **Unverified; do not teach or publish as established stems** |

No independently sourced present-tense example, past-tense transitive sentence, imperative, or full agreement paradigm for **xewandin** was found in the consulted material. Do not invent forms such as *dixewînim*, *xewand*, or a patient-agreement table from the derivational spelling alone.

## 3. Northern Kurmanji comparison

For the Northern Kurmanji / Kurmanjî pathway, the more securely established ordinary verbs meaning “sleep” are:
- **razan**
- **nivistin**
- **raketin**

Glosbe lists these as the main Kurmanji translations of English “sleep”, with **xewtin** only among less frequent translations. The Northern Kurdish cross-reference on Wiktionary's Central Kurdish *xewtin* page is **xeftin**. These sources do not establish a standard Northern Kurmanji factitive *xewandin*.

This is not a claim that no Northern Kurmanji speaker ever uses **xewtin** or **xewandin**. It is a narrower evidence judgment: the sources reviewed do not establish the pair as part of the target standard or provide the conjugation evidence needed for a course entry.

## 4. Teaching-safe decision

**Do not add xewtin → xewandin to the Standard Northern Kurmanji factitive list at this stage.**

If retained in research documentation, label it:
- **Sulaimani / Central Kurdish (Sorani) or Lower-Kurmanji terminology in the cited article**
- **xewtin** — intransitive “sleep”, present stem **xew**, attested past example **xewt**
- **xewandin** — noun-derived causative “put to sleep”, dialect-attributed to Lower Kurmanji/Sorani by Deham
- transitive stems and conjugation: **not established from the consulted evidence**

For Standard Northern Kurmanji learner content, use a separately verified form such as **razan / razandin** or **nivistin / nivandin**, while keeping the dialectal *xewtin / xewandin* note out of the core paradigm.
