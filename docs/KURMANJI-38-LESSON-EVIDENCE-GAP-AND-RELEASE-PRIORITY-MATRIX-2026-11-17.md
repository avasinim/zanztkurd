## Executive conclusion

The repository contains source-review coverage for all 38 lessons, but coverage is not equivalent to certification. Existing reviews explicitly leave exact examples, dialect scope, IPA/orthography presentation, Sorani glosses, or assessment design unresolved in multiple places. The current evidence supports a staged editorial process, not a blanket claim that all lessons are scientifically approved.

This matrix consolidates the existing reviews into a practical order of work. It does not re-review every underlying linguistic claim from primary sources, and it does not authorize lesson edits or migration execution.

## 2. Cross-course priority queue

### P0 — correctness and scope risks

1. Orthography inventory and RR (Course 1 L02/L04/L26): maintain the 31-letter Hawar inventory; never count RR as a separate 32nd letter. Separate the phonetic tap/trill distinction from orthographic policy and cite the exact source convention.
2. Case and ergativity (Course 1 L11/L20/L23/L25; Course 2 L03/L04/L07/L11): maintain consistent Direct/Oblique terminology. Do not equate Direct with subject or Oblique with object. Explicitly scope canonical past-transitive alignment and dialect variation.
3. Ezafe and nominal morphology (Course 1 L21/L24; Course 2 L03–06): distinguish head-noun gender/number agreement, case, oblique possessor marking, linking ezafe and free/demonstrative ezafe.
4. Verb TAM and negation (Course 1 L17/L18; Course 2 L08–10): separate stems, agreement, indicative/subjunctive, future particles and negation prefixes. Keep Standard Kurmanji and regional patterns distinct.
5. Exact example verification: do not present instructor-created examples as direct source quotations. Label source-attested, adapted and newly authored examples separately.

### P1 — linguistic evidence and example coverage

6. Pinpoint source sections/pages for Course 1 L01 definitions, L04 consonant/IPA claims, L09 place-name inflections, L13 counted-object/duration sentences, L14 object vocabulary, L16 time sentences, L19 interrogatives, L20 fronting, L21 feminine indefinite ezafe, and L22 comparative variants.
7. Expand Course 2's worked paradigms where the lesson title promises a grammatical category but the current body offers only abstract definitions.
8. Check all lexical items against a specialist Kurmanji dictionary or source corpus; a general teaching page is not enough to certify every gloss.

### P2 — editorial and assessment

9. Run a separate native Sorani copy-edit across all 38 lessons. Kurmanji grammar sources do not validate Sorani translation choices.
10. Decide and document the Avasin Sorani house spelling before normalizing Sorani prose. Do not transfer Sorani editorial preferences into Kurmanji Latin spellings.
11. Make assessment claims match the actual learner experience: distinguish review, self-assessment and scored test; define answer keys, scoring and learning outcomes where a test is claimed.

## 3. Source hierarchy for the next review pass

Use sources according to the claim type:

1. Descriptive grammar and peer-reviewed research — primary authority for case, agreement, ezafe, morphology, TAM, syntax and dialect variation. Haig (2018), Haig & Öpengin (2015/2018), Gündoğdu (2015), and Baker & Atlamaz (2014) are already cited in the existing reviews.
2. Specialist phonetics/orthography descriptions — for grapheme inventory, IPA values, aspiration, pharyngealization, tap/trill and the scope of RR.
3. Specialist dictionaries and corpus attestations — for individual lexical entries, inflected place names, idioms, greetings and example sentences.
4. Teaching resources — useful corroboration for beginner phrase choices, but not sufficient by themselves for a full grammatical generalization.
5. Unicode and database standards — for NFC, normalization, collation and digital search claims; linguistic references cannot establish software behavior.
6. Native Sorani editorial review — a separate authority for Sorani explanations, glosses, spelling and punctuation.

For each claim, record exact source title, edition/year, section/page or stable URL anchor, quotation or faithful paraphrase, variety/register, and whether the lesson example is quoted, adapted or instructor-created. Do not invent page numbers or source attributions.

## 4. Recommended review workflow

1. Start with P0 claims; record precise evidence and variety scope without changing lesson text.
2. Verify examples one by one, including inflection and intended meaning, not just dictionary headwords.
3. Create a separate Sorani editorial issue list; do not merge it with Kurmanji grammar findings.
4. Mark each claim SUPPORTED, PARTLY SUPPORTED, UNRESOLVED, or EDITORIAL, with evidence locator and reviewer confidence.
5. Only after the review record is complete, prepare a minimal proposed lesson patch and a separate before/after diff for review.
6. Re-run lesson package, seed parity, browser and accessibility checks after any approved content change.
7. Keep the historical release migration immutable; any approved data correction must be a new forward migration with a verified precondition and disposable-database test.
