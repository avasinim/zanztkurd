# Source-language correction: Thackston PDF is Sorani, not Kurmanji (2026-11-17)

## Correction
The PDF hosted at https://kurdishcentral.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Thackstons-Grammar.pdf is titled *Sorani Kurdish: A Reference Grammar with Selected Readings* by W. M. Thackston. Its contents explicitly identify §27 as “The Simple Past (Transitive): The Ergative,” §30.1 as “The Past Perfect Tense (Transitive),” §33 as “The Past Conditional,” and the whole work as Sorani Kurdish. It is therefore not a Kurmanji source.

## Consequence for prior Kurmanji-medium audits
Any claim in project notes that attributes Kurmanji paradigms or Kurmanji past conditional / past perfect conditional forms to this specific PDF is invalid as Kurmanji evidence and must not be used to establish Academy Kurmanji rules. The PDF may be useful for Sorani comparison only, clearly labelled as such.

This correction does not assert that the general ergative pattern is absent in Kurmanji. Independent Kurmanji references support ergative alignment in past transitive clauses, with an oblique transitive subject and direct object, and verb agreement with the direct object. See Haig (2018), §2.7.2, and the Kurmanji learner grammar examples of `xwendin` in the simple past. These sources do not by themselves establish a complete Kurmanji past conditional paradigm for `xwendin`.

## Evidence
1. The PDF's own title page says “Sorani Kurdish — A Reference Grammar” and names W. M. Thackston.
2. Its contents list §27 “The Simple Past (Transitive): The Ergative,” §30.1 “The Past Perfect Tense (Transitive),” and §33 “The Past Conditional.”
3. Haig (2018), “Northern Kurdish (Kurmanjî),” §2.7.2, independently describes Kurmanji ergative alignment in past transitive clauses: the transitive subject is oblique, the direct object is direct, and the verb agrees with the direct object.
4. The Kurmanji Lesson 7 grammar directly identifies `xwendin` past stem `xwend` and explains that past transitive verb endings agree with the object, not the subject. It provides simple-past forms, not the past conditional.

## Editorial action
- Mark the cited Thackston PDF as Sorani-only in Kurmanji source reviews.
- Re-audit Kurmanji past conditional and past perfect conditional forms using Kurmanji-specific sources.
- Keep the Kurmanji transitive object-agreement principle, but cite Kurmanji sources for it.
- Do not extrapolate `xwendin` conditional forms or publish them as Academy standard without direct evidence.
- No learner-facing lesson, course inventory, dialect policy, code, CI, runtime, seed, database, migration, PR #3, or deployment is changed.
