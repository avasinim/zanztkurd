# Xwendin: singular/plural object agreement and subject-agreement variation audit

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only grammar audit for the Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway.

## Executive finding

The past transitive verb **xwendin** (“read; study”) follows the canonical object-agreement pattern:
- With a third-person singular direct object, the past form is **xwend** (no overt agreement suffix).
- With a third-person plural direct object, the past form can be **xwendin** (plural agreement **-in**).

A directly attested contrast in Thackston's *Kurmanji Kurdish* is:
- **Me ew helbest xwend.** — “We read that poem.”
- **Me ew helbest xwendin.** — “We read those poems.”

The plural marking on **xwendin** corresponds to the plural patient/object **ew helbest** (“those poems”), not simply to the plural agent **me** (“we”). The example is especially valuable because the agent is identical in both clauses while object number changes.

## 1. Source-attested minimal contrast

Thackston §18.3 gives the two sentences above and explains that the plural object is indicated only by the plural form of the verb:
- Singular object: **ew helbest** (“that poem”) → **xwend**.
- Plural object: **ew helbest** (“those poems”, plural is contextually understood) → **xwendin**.

In this noun phrase the unmodified plural noun does not carry an overt plural ending; the verbal agreement marks the plurality. Do not infer that every plural object in every dialect must be expressed only on the verb: this is a specific documented construction and source example.

## 2. Separate object agreement from agent number

The two examples keep **me** constant:
- **Me** = first-person plural oblique agent (“we”).
- The object changes from singular to plural.
- The verb changes from **xwend** to **xwendin**.

This minimal contrast supports teaching the default rule: the past transitive verb agrees with the direct object.

Do not teach **xwendin** as a plural-agent ending simply because **me** is plural. That would misanalyse the pair.

## 3. Person agreement with pronominal objects

Kurdîzan's lesson on past transitive object agreement gives a transparent general pattern with **dîtin**:
- **Te ez dîtim.** — you saw me; **-im** agrees with direct object **ez**.
- **Min tu dîtî.** — I saw you; **-î** agrees with direct object **tu**.
- **Me ew dît.** — we saw him/her; third-person singular object has no ending.
- **Wê em dîtin.** — she saw us; **-in** agrees with plural direct object **em**.

These are not xwendin conjugation examples; they corroborate the general agreement rule and must be labelled as **dîtin** examples if used in instruction. Do not present them as a full paradigm of **xwendin**.

## 4. Important qualification: plural-agent agreement variation

Northern Kurdish grammar documents a variation: when the transitive agent is plural, particularly when not overtly repeated in a coordinated discourse, plural agreement can appear even when the direct object is singular. The occurrence depends on dialect, discourse, and other factors. This is a documented departure from the canonical object-agreement pattern, not a reason to erase the basic rule.

For the Academy's beginner Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway:
- Teach object agreement as the default.
- Keep plural-agent agreement as a separate advanced variation note.
- Do not claim that every past transitive verb form in all Kurmanji varieties is controlled only by the object without exception.
- Do not import regional double-oblique object constructions into the default model.

## 5. Teaching-safe table

| Past transitive construction | Direct object | Attested verb | Agreement interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Me ew helbest xwend.** | singular “that poem” | **xwend** | third-person singular object; zero ending |
| **Me ew helbest xwendin.** | plural “those poems” | **xwendin** | third-person plural object; **-in** |
| **Wê em dîtin.** | **em** “us” (plural) | **dîtin** | plural pronominal object; **-in** |

The first two are source examples with **xwendin**. The third is a separate corroborating example with **dîtin**.

## 6. Teaching-safe decision

For the Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway:
1. Use the pair **Me ew helbest xwend / Me ew helbest xwendin** to teach singular vs. plural object agreement.
2. Explain that the same oblique agent **me** occurs in both sentences, so the verb alternation is not agreement with the agent.
3. Label the plural ending **-in** as object-number agreement in this example.
4. Use **dîtin** pronominal-object examples only as separate corroboration, not as forms of **xwendin**.
5. Introduce plural-agent agreement variation only in an advanced note, with its discourse and dialect constraints.
6. Do not modify learner-facing lessons until explicit approval.
