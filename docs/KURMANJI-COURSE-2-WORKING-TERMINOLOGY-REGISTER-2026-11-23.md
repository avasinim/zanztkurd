# Course 2 — Working Terminology Register (Standard Kurmanjî)
Date: 2026-11-23
Status: Editorial control document; proposed register for review, not an authorization to rewrite learner-facing lessons.

## Purpose and rule of use
This register consolidates terminology decisions from the Course 2 audits. It is intended to keep the 12 lessons internally consistent. It does not replace linguistic sources, and it does not authorize changing any Kurmanjî example, transcription, gloss or translation. Where the register marks a term as provisional, retain the source wording in existing lessons until the course owner approves a choice.

## A. Core morphology terms

| Concept | Preferred Sorani label | Working definition | Editorial rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| morphology (discipline) | مۆرفۆلۆجی | Study of word structure and formation | Do not use as a synonym for a morpheme. |
| morpheme | مۆرفیم | Smallest grammatical unit with meaning or function | Keep distinct from مۆرفۆلۆجی. |
| root | ڕەگ | Core lexical element from which a word is built | Do not automatically equate with base or stem. |
| base | بنەڕەت | Form to which a morphological operation applies | May contain more than a root; define in each analysis. |
| stem | ستێم (provisional) | Form used as the base for inflection | Provisional label only; owner/editor must approve the Sorani equivalent and scope before learner-facing standardization. |
| prefix | پێشگر | Morpheme attached before a base | Use only where segmentation is source-supported. |
| suffix | پاشگر | Morpheme attached after a base | Distinguish suffixes by function, not merely by spelling. |
| inflection | گۆڕانی ڕێزمانی (provisional) | Form change expressing grammatical information | Preferred Sorani label requires owner/editor confirmation. |
| derivation | وشەسازیی وەچەیی (provisional) | Formation of a new lexeme or lexical meaning by morphology | Do not conflate with inflection; Sorani label remains open. |

## B. Nominal morphology and phrase structure

| Concept | Preferred Sorani label | Working definition | Editorial rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| noun | ناو | Nominal lexical category | Distinguish from noun phrase. |
| grammatical gender | ڕەگەزی ڕێزمانی | Grammatical noun class reflected in agreement/forms | Do not imply biological sex. |
| number | ژمارە | Singular/plural grammatical distinction | State where the source paradigm has syncretism or neutralization. |
| definiteness | دیارێتی | Whether a referent is presented as definite | Explain using context, not a suffix-only shortcut. |
| indefiniteness | نادیارێتی | Whether a referent is presented as indefinite | Keep separate from oblique case. |
| indefinite marker -ek | پاشگری نادیاریی -ek | Indefinite marker in the relevant nominal paradigm | Preserve Latin form exactly; do not Sorani-ize or respell. |
| ezafe | ئەزافە | Linking construction/marker relating a head noun to a modifier or possessor | Identify its attachment and agreement with the head noun. |
| head noun | ناوی سەرەکی | Noun that heads the phrase and controls relevant ezafe agreement | Use consistently in phrase analysis. |
| possessor | خاوەندار | Phrase or pronoun expressing possession/association | In relevant Kurmanjî constructions, check its case marking from the source. |
| adjective | هاوەڵناو | Modifier describing a noun | Do not claim it carries the noun's case ending unless source evidence supports it. |
| possessive pronoun | جێناوی خاوەنداری (provisional) | Pronoun/form expressing possession | Lesson 6 scope should be checked against its actual examples. |
| reflexive pronoun | جێناوی خۆیی (provisional) | Form referring back to an antecedent | Explain the scope of `xwe` with a verified example; do not generalize beyond same-clause evidence. |

## C. Case, pronouns and alignment

| Concept | Preferred Sorani label | Working definition | Editorial rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| case | حاڵەت | Grammatical marking/distribution of noun phrases and pronouns | Do not equate case directly with semantic role. |
| direct case | حاڵەتی ڕاستەوخۆ | Direct-form case in the Kurmanjî paradigm | Not simply “subject case”; explain construction-specific distribution. |
| oblique case | حاڵەتی ناڕاستەوخۆ | Oblique-form case in the Kurmanjî paradigm | Not simply “object case”; include possessors and adposition complements where relevant. |
| personal pronoun | جێناوی کەسی | Pronoun referring to speech participants or other referents | Present direct/oblique forms together when teaching case. |
| syncretism | یەک‌شێوەییی فۆرم (provisional) | One form serving more than one grammatical value | Explain `ew` explicitly rather than duplicating it without a note. |
| transitive verb | کرداری تێپەڕ | Verb with a direct object in the relevant construction | Keep distinct from ergative alignment. |
| intransitive verb | کرداری ناتێپەڕ | Verb without a direct object in the relevant construction | Do not infer case pattern from verb label alone. |
| ergative alignment | ڕێکخستنی ئەرجەتیڤ (provisional) | Alignment pattern in which past transitive subjects show oblique marking in the described Kurmanjî system | Define with a complete sourced clause; do not call it a word-order rule. |
| agreement | ڕێککەوتنی ڕێزمانی | Matching of grammatical features between related elements | Identify exactly which element agrees with which. |

## D. Verbs, time and clause structure

| Concept | Preferred Sorani label | Working definition | Editorial rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| verb | کردار | Lexical category expressing an action, event or state | Distinguish verb root from inflected verb form. |
| person | کەس | First, second or third grammatical person | Use same labels across pronoun and verb paradigms. |
| tense | کات | Grammatical location in time | Keep distinct from aspect. |
| aspect | ڕوانگەی کردار (provisional) | How an event is viewed internally, e.g. ongoing or bounded | Sorani label requires owner/editor confirmation; do not collapse into tense. |
| present | ئێستا | Present-time form/category | Describe only the source-attested construction. |
| past | ڕابردوو | Past-time form/category | Distinguish simple past from imperfective past where relevant. |
| future | داهاتوو | Future-time form/category | Preserve source form and contextual gloss; avoid one-form universal claims. |
| negation | نەفی | Grammatical negation | Teach the source-attested patterns with their limits and exceptions. |
| yes/no question | پرسیاری بەڵێ/نەخێر | Question expecting a yes/no response | Verify particle and prosody against the target Standard Kurmanjî scope. |
| question word | وشەی پرسیار | Interrogative form requesting a particular piece of information | Use source-checked Kurmanjî forms and glosses. |
| word order | ڕیزبەندیی وشەکان | Ordering of clause constituents | Do not present one neutral pattern as an exceptionless fixed order. |
| SOVG | SOVG (ڕێکخستنی بکەر–بەرکار–کردار–ئامانج) | Haig's notation for neutral order with a goal argument | Explain G as goal/recipient/addressee; do not reduce it to plain SOV. |
| focus/fronting | جەختکردنەوە بە پێشخستن (provisional) | Pragmatic placement of an element toward the clause beginning | Treat as information structure, not as proof that neutral order is different. |

## E. Cross-course style and source rules
1. Write **بڕگە** for “syllable”; do not substitute هجا.
2. If a Sorani color term is needed, write **سەبز**.
3. Keep all Kurmanjî Latin forms exactly as they appear in the approved source unless a correction is independently verified and authorized.
4. Do not create Kurmanjî examples to fill a teaching gap. Use source-attested examples or mark the gap for author-supplied material.
5. Every example used to teach morphology should, where possible, include segmentation, grammatical labels, gloss and translation.
6. A morpheme label must not be inferred from a similar-looking string alone. The same visible ending can serve different functions in different constructions.
7. Use Geoffrey Haig (2018) as the principal source for the grammatical analyses already reviewed. Use Badıllı (1965) only as historical/regional corroboration, not as a universal Standard Kurmanjî authority.
8. Keep dialect variation explicit. Do not silently merge forms from different varieties.
