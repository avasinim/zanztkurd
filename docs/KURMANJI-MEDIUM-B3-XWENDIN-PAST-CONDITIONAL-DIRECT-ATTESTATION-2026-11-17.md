# Direct attestation found for xwendin past conditional (2026-11-17)

## New source finding
A full-text inspection of W. M. Thackston, *Kurmanji Kurdish: A Reference Grammar with Selected Readings*, §25.1, found an exact **xwendin** past-conditional form in the prose example on printed p. 65:

> Lê wî biryara xwe dabû, wî ê bixwenda.
> “But he had made his decision: he would study.”

This directly attests **bixwenda** as a Kurmanji past-conditional form of **xwendin** in a third-person singular context. It is not merely a form projected from the paradigm. Source:
https://www.academia.edu/7363808/Kurmanji_Kurdish_A_Reference_Grammar_with_Selected_Readings
The relevant §25.1 passage and example are visible in the extracted source at lines 263–270 and 283–284.

## What this changes
- The previous research note's statement that no independent source directly attested the full xwendin paradigm remains accurate, but it should be refined: **one exact xwendin form, bixwenda, is directly attested in Thackston §25.1**.
- The other object-agreement forms previously projected for the transitive paradigm (`wî bixwendama`, `wî bixwendayî`, `wî bixwendana`, and their negative counterparts) remain morphological projections from the consonant-final stem pattern and the `dîtin` paradigm. They are not directly attested as a complete xwendin paradigm in the inspected passage.
- The exact phrase `wî ê bixwenda` contains a future/modal particle `ê` followed by the past conditional. The attested verb form itself is `bixwenda`. Preserve this context and do not mislabel the entire phrase as a simple bare conditional paradigm.
- Thackston §25.1 states that past conditionals of transitive verbs are ergative; its paradigm shows object agreement with a constant oblique agent. This supports the structural derivation but does not turn unattested xwendin person forms into direct quotations.

## Independent description of the pattern
Manova, “Negation in Kurmanji,” summarizes Thackston's past conditional as a circumfixal pattern with **bi-** plus past stem and suffixes. For consonant-final stems, it lists:
- 1sg: `bi-stem-ama`
- 2sg: `bi-stem-ayî`
- 3sg: `bi-stem-a`
- plural: `bi-stem-ana`

Source:
https://www.academia.edu/3342645/Negation_in_Kurmanji_In_Manova_ed_Affix_Ordering_Across_Languages_and_Frameworks_Oxford_University_Press

Wiktionary's Kurmanji entry independently lists the infinitive `xwendin` and past stem `xwend-`:
https://ku.wiktionary.org/wiki/xwendin
This confirms the stem used for morphological analysis, not the whole conditional paradigm.
