# Kurmanji conditional source recovery and correction of Sorani/Kurmanji attribution (2026-11-17)

## Important correction
The earlier source-language correction correctly identified the specific PDF at `kurdishcentral.org/.../Thackstons-Grammar.pdf` as *Sorani Kurdish: A Reference Grammar with Selected Readings*. However, it must not be read as saying that Thackston has no separate Kurmanji grammar. A distinct work exists: W. M. Thackston, *Kurmanji Kurdish: A Reference Grammar with Selected Readings*. Its title page, contents, and preface identify it as Kurmanji, and its contents include §§25.1–25.2 on past conditional and past perfect conditionals.

## Direct Kurmanji evidence: Thackston §§25.1–25.2
The Kurmanji edition is available at:
- https://agorism.dev/book/kurdish/kurmanji_1_grammar.pdf
- https://www.academia.edu/7363808/Kurmanji_Kurdish_A_Reference_Grammar_with_Selected_Readings

### §25.1 Past conditional
Thackston states that the past conditional is formed with **bí-** plus the past stem and person/object endings; the preverb may be omitted in close compounds. It explicitly states that transitive past conditionals are ergative.

Direct transitive paradigm examples:
- `wî bidîtama`
- `wî bidîtayî`
- `wî bidîta`
- `wî bidîtana`
Negative examples include `wî nedîtama` and `wî nedîtana`.

These examples are for **dîtin**, not **xwendin**. They demonstrate object agreement in the transitive conditional: the ending changes with the direct object, while the oblique agent `wî` remains constant.

### §25.2 Past perfect conditionals
Thackston identifies two dialectally mutually exclusive varieties:
1. **bí-** affirmative / **né-** negative + past perfect + `-(y)a`.
2. **bí-** or **né-** + past perfect with `ba` replacing `bû`.

Direct transitive **dîtin** examples include:
- `min bídîtibûya`, `te bídîtibûya`, `wî bídîtibûya`, `me bídîtibûya`, `we bídîtibûya`, `wan bídîtibûya`.
- Negative: `min nédîtibûya`, `te nédîtibûya`, `wî nédîtibûya`, `me nédîtibûya`, `we nédîtibûya`, `wan nédîtibûya`.
- Second variety: `min bídîtiba`, `te bídîtiba`, `wî bídîtiba`, `me bídîtiba`, `we bídîtiba`, `wan bídîtiba`, with corresponding `né-` negative forms.

The source marks the two varieties as dialectally exclusive; do not combine them into one paradigm or imply both are used by every speaker.

## Independent corroboration
Deniz Ekici, *Kurmanji Kurdish Reader* (2007), Part 1, IX.B “Past Tense Subjunctive,” pp. 98–99, directly presents a past subjunctive formation and transitive **dîtin** paradigm, with endings `-ama, -ayî, -a, -ana`. Its terminology and paradigm are not identical in scope to Thackston's §25.1, so preserve the source labels rather than collapsing the two analyses.

## Application to xwendin
- The recovered Kurmanji Thackston source now directly establishes the general transitive past conditional pattern and past perfect conditional variants, using **dîtin**.
- It does not supply a complete **xwendin** conditional paradigm.
- Any constructed **xwendin** forms remain extrapolations. Do not publish them as directly attested or as Academy-standard until checked with additional Kurmanji-specific evidence and the Academy's selected variety.
- The earlier object-agreement principle remains supported by Kurmanji evidence; the source citation must be the Kurmanji edition, not the Sorani PDF.
