## Coverage compared with source
The payload includes the lesson's framing and all 12 principal comparison headings:
1. Definition and geographic/linguistic scope of Southern Kurdish; Feyli, Kalhuri and Kirmaşani are subsets, not interchangeable labels. It distinguishes Southern Kurdish from Hewrami, Leki and Luri and warns against treating all Southern Iraq/Kurdistan speech as one variety.
2. Kurmanji B versus Southern W, with baran/waran, kitêb/kitaw, xirab/xiraw, xerîb/xerîw; native versus Arabic-loan strata and historical B/P > V/W correspondences.
3. Kurmanji V versus Southern W/M, with çav, mêvan, nav, zava and zevî; historical M correspondence.
4. Kurmanji Û versus Southern Ü/Î, with zû, dûr, rû, mû and eastern Kurmanji/Behdinî variants.
5. Kurmanji O versus Southern Û, with roj/rûj, zor/zûr, kor/kûr and dew/dû, including the author's vowel-shift explanation and eastern Kurmanji parallels.
6. Southern RD versus Kurmanji R, with birdin, kirdin, mirdin, pird, sard and xwardin.
7. Light L versus dark Ł, with keçel/keçeł, dil/dił, gul/guł, bela/beła, and the Kirmaşan/Mendelî exception.
8. Aspiration of Ç/K/P/T and the distinction from emphatic/heavy consonants.
9. Initial ST cluster simplification/prothesis, including standin/sendin, stêr/estêre/hesare, plus medial/final examples dest/des, şêst/şeṣ, zivistan/zimsan and Kurdistan/Kursan.
10. A/EH versus YE, with kanî/kyenî, tehl/tyeł and nizanim/nyezanim.
11. NG versus Ň [ŋ], with deng/deň, reng/reň and bang/baň.
12. XWE-/XUE- in Kurmanji and Southern Kurdish versus XO- in Sorani, with xwe/xo and xweş/xoş.

## Findings and qualifications
- The broad section structure and its main examples are present; no major numbered topic from printed pp.387–393 was found absent in the payload.
- The source's geographic description and dialect labels are sensitive to classification. The payload keeps the author's framing and does not independently assert a universal classification.

- Table typography and rare diacritics (especially zeẅ/zeü, Ł/ł, Ň/ň, and emphatic S) should receive a final visual check in the rendered lesson before release.
