# Şemzînan Prospective Negation: Locality-Tagged Attestation and Limits

Date: 2026-11-17  
Type: documentation-only evidence addendum to the Şemzînan morphology audit.

## Direct Şemzînan form

Haig & Öpengin, “Kurmanji Kurdish in Turkey: Structure, varieties and status,” §4.1.1, give the following entry in their table of Şemzînan phonological/morphological examples:

*na-č-i* — NEG-go.PRS-2SG — ‘Won’t you go?’

This is directly labelled Şemzînan and is important because it provides a locality-tagged negative indicative form with a prospective (‘won’t you go?’) interpretation. It is stronger locality-specific evidence than the Behdinî declarative sentence *Ez sibe digel wan na-çi-m* recorded in the separate future-negation audit.

## What this does and does not establish

- Directly attested: Şemzînan *na-č-i*, glossed NEG-go.PRS-2SG, translated by the source as ‘Won’t you go?’
- Supported interpretation: a negated present-indicative form can receive a prospective reading in this interrogative context.
- Not directly attested here: a declarative sentence equivalent to ‘I will not go tomorrow’ specifically from a Şemzînan speaker.
- Therefore do not convert *na-č-i* into a declarative future example, and do not treat this one question as a complete negative-future paradigm.
- Read together with Haig (2018), §2.3, which explicitly generalizes that in Şemzînan and northern-Iraq Kurmanji varieties the Standard-Kurmanji future-particle + negated-subjunctive construction is unavailable and a negated indicative is used instead, this direct question is compatible with the regional analysis. The generalization and the locality-tagged sentence are different kinds of evidence and should remain separately labelled.

## Teaching and documentation boundary

This evidence can support a research note explaining prospective interpretation of negative indicative forms. It is not sufficient to prescribe a full Şemzînan future-negation lesson or an Academy-wide rule. Do not change existing lessons, dialect policy, or the Kurmanji-medium pathway without explicit review and approval.
