## Coverage verified

The authored lesson represents the following source material:

1. **Scope and naming of Southern Kurdish (p. 387):** the group is distinguished from the whole of Southern Kurdistan and from Hawrami/Gorani, Laki and Luri; Feyli, Kalhuri and Kirmashani are treated as parts rather than synonyms for the whole group. The source's non-hierarchical linguistic comparison and its note about ergativity are retained.
2. **Kurmanji B / Southern Kurdish W (pp. 388–389):** examples *baran/waran*, *kitêb/kitaw*, *xirab/xiraw*, *xerîb/xerîw*; native versus Arabic-loanword histories; post-vocalic B/P developments, including Pahlavi *sêp*, Persian *sêb*, Kurmanji *sêv*, Sorani *sêw*, and the source's Serhed/Central Anatolia caveat (*kitêw, kitêv*).
3. **Kurmanji V / Southern Kurdish W or M (pp. 388–389):** *çav/çaw*, *mêvan/mîwan*, *nav/naw*, *zava/zawa*, *zevî/zewî*, including dialectal *zeẅ/zeü* and the historical M explanation.
4. **Kurmanji Û / Southern Kurdish Ü, Î or retained Û (p. 389):** *zû, dûr, rû, mû* and the source's dialect variation. The Behdînî comparison and forms *bük/bwîk*, *dür/dwîr*, *zü/zwî*, *mü/mwî*, and Duhok-area *bîk, dîr, zî, mî* are retained.
5. **Kurmanji O / Southern Kurdish Û (p. 390):** *roj/rûj*, *zor/zûr*, *kor/kûr*, *(dew)/do/dû*, with the source's vowel-shift explanation and eastern Kurmanji *gût/gut* comparison.
6. **Southern Kurdish RD / Kurmanji R (pp. 390–391):** all six source comparisons, including *birdin/birin*, *kirdin/kirin*, *mirdin/mirin*, *pird/pir*, *sard/sar* and *xwardin/xwarin*.
7. **L / Ł (p. 391):** *keçel/keçeł*, *dil/dił*, *gul/guł*, *bela/beła*, the Kirmashan/Mendeli exception, and the source's distinction between native and Arabic-loanword historical origins.
8. **Ç/K/P/T aspiration (p. 391):** initial aspirated versus medial/final unaspirated distribution, with the source's qualification.
9. **ST / S, ES, HES (pp. 391–392):** initial cluster avoidance and the contrast with Sorani, including *standin/sendin*, *stêr/estêre/hesare*, *dest/des*, *şêst/şeṣ*, *zivistan/zimsan* and *Kurdistan/Kursan*. The source's note that Kurmanji preserves ST across positions is represented.
10. **A/EH / YE (p. 392):** *kanî/kyenî*, *tehl/tyeł*, *nizanim/nyezanim*, including the source's footnote relating *nazanim* to *nakim, naxwim, nabêjim*.
11. **NG / Ň (p. 393):** the [ŋ] explanation and *deng/deň*, *reng/reň*, *bang/baň*; the Sorani qualification is retained.
12. **XWE / XO (p. 393):** *xwe/xo* and *xweş/xoş*, with the historical direction stated by the source.
