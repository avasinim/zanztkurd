# Şemzînan Negative-Future Evidence: Corpus Access and Attestation Boundary

Date: 2026-11-17  
Type: documentation-only research boundary note.

## Current directly supported evidence

1. Haig & Öpengin's description of Şemzînan gives *na-č-i*, glossed NEG-go.PRS-2SG and translated “Won't you go?” This is a locality-labelled interrogative example with prospective interpretation.
2. Haig (2018), §2.3, states that the Standard Kurmanji future particle plus negated subjunctive construction is unavailable in Şemzînan and northern-Iraq varieties, and that a negated indicative is used for negative future.
3. The declarative Behdinî example *Ez sibe digel wan na-çi-m* (“I won't go with them tomorrow”) is directly labelled Behdinî. It is not a Şemzînan quotation.

## Missing evidence

No directly locality-labelled Şemzînan declarative negative-future sentence equivalent to “I will not go tomorrow” has been located in the sources examined for this audit. The interrogative *na-č-i* cannot be silently converted into such a declarative example. No full person paradigm should be projected from it.

## Corpus route and access constraint

The University of Bamberg's Corpus of Contemporary Written Kurdish (CCWK), compiled by Abdullah Incekan and Geoffrey Haig (2021), contains more than 900,000 words of contemporary written Northern Kurdish, predominantly fiction, with some non-fiction. The corpus is not tagged or translated. Its data are available only on request because of copyright constraints; the corpus description directs researchers to contact Geoffrey Haig for access.

The corpus is described as contemporary written Northern Kurdish and is not, on the public description alone, a locality-tagged Şemzînan corpus. Even if access is granted, a text occurrence must be independently attributable to a Şemzînan speaker, author, or locality before it can count as direct Şemzînan evidence. Standardized written Kurmanji text alone is not enough.

## Safe next research steps

- Seek authorized access to CCWK through its stated academic contact route; no contact has been sent as part of this repository task.
- If access is granted, search candidate negative indicative forms only as discovery leads, then inspect the full context and source provenance.
- Record author/speaker, locality, genre, date, orthography, clause type, and future context for every candidate.
- Reject examples whose locality cannot be established, or label them only as general written Kurmanji.
- Seek a published Şemzînan narrative, interview, or elicitation transcript with an explicit future context if the corpus cannot establish locality.
- Keep interrogative prospective examples, declarative future examples, and broad areal generalizations in separate evidence categories.

## Curriculum and policy boundary

This note does not establish a complete Şemzînan negative-future paradigm, does not authorize a new lesson, and does not change Academy language policy or existing course content. It is a research record only.

## Audit conclusion

The evidence supports the regional grammatical generalization and one direct Şemzînan prospective negative-indicative question. It does not yet supply a verified Şemzînan declarative negative-future quotation. Preserve this distinction until a provenance-checked primary text is available.
