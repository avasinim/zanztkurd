# Course 2 Lesson 01 — Instructional Audit (2026-11-23)

## Scope
Review of current seed lesson 01, "مۆرفۆلۆجی و پێکهاتەی وشە", for teaching clarity and example coverage. Documentation only; no lesson source, SQL, application code, database, Supabase, main, or production changes.

## Current coverage
1. Word vs. morpheme definition.
2. Distinguishing root, derivational morpheme and grammatical marker.
3. `-ek` as an indefinite singular suffix, deferred to Lesson 04.
4. Review questions and references.

## Instructional gaps (not confirmed linguistic errors)
- **No worked segmentation:** Students are told to distinguish root, word-building morphemes and grammatical markers, but are not shown a complete worked example with labels and reasoning.
- **Definition contrast is abstract:** The difference between an independent word and a bound morpheme could be shown with a paired example.
- **-ek is deferred:** Mentioning it briefly without an annotated example is appropriate as a preview, but students may not yet see what “grammatical function” means.
- **Review questions lack a model answer:** Three questions exist, but no short sample response or checklist indicates what a correct explanation should include.
- **Term consistency:** Lesson uses “ڕەگ” and “بنەڕەت”; Lesson 02 already notes these can be distinct in some analyses. Lesson 01 could preview this distinction without treating terminology as universal.

## Suggested additions for a later approved content pass
- Add one simple, source-verified Kurmanji example and visually segment it into base/root plus marker, with gloss and explanation. Choose a form also covered by Lesson 02/04 to maintain consistency.
- Add a paired contrast: a free word versus a bound suffix, explicitly noting why the suffix cannot stand alone.
- Add a compact “what to identify” model: form, segmentation, function, and evidence.
- Add a short answer key or model response for one review question.

## Source gate
The current lesson references Haig (2018) and Thackston (2006), but this audit did not independently re-verify an exact worked segmentation example from those sources. No new example is proposed as a fact. Source check remains necessary before adding examples.

## Conclusion
The lesson has a clear introductory structure and no error confirmed in this focused review. The primary improvement is pedagogical: it needs a worked example and a small answer model so the abstract definition is operational. This is a teaching-gap audit, not a full linguistic validation.
