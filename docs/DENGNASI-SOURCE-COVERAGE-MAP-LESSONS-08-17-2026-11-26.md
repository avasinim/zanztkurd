## Important limitation

This document records source-page mapping and structural/topic coverage, not a line-by-line proof that every example, footnote, table cell and historical qualification from printed pages 68–250 has been retained. The authoring text for Lessons 8–17 is substantially detailed and the major source headings are represented, but a deeper example-by-example reconciliation remains open. No blanket claim that these ten lessons are fully exhaustive is made.
