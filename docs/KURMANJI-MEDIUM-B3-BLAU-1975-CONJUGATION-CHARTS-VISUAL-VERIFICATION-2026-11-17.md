# Blau (1975): Visual Verification of Conjugation Charts, Printed pp. 90–96 — 2026-11-17

## Scope

This addendum follows the primary-text evidence audit for Joyce Blau (1975). It records a visual inspection of the original scanned chart images, rather than relying on OCR alone. The charts are locality-labelled and must not be merged into a single generic “Badini” paradigm.

Primary scan: Kurdipedia document viewer, 260 pages. Printed pages 90–96 correspond to viewer pages 93–99 (zero-indexed PDF page numbers 93–99 are not the printed page numbers; see page map below).

## 1. Verified chart inventory

The printed chart index on p. 87 lists:
- p. 88: Amêdî personal endings.
- p. 89: Sincarî personal endings.
- p. 90: kirin/ke- “do” in Amêdî.
- p. 91: kirin/k- “do” in Sincarî.
- p. 92: k'eft'in/k'ev- “fall” in Amêdî.
- p. 93: k'etin/k'ev- “fall” in Sincarî.
- p. 94: hat'in/hê- “come” in Amêdî.
- p. 95: hatin/ê- “come” in Sincarî.
- p. 96: variant present stem of hat'in/hê-/ê-.

This chart inventory itself establishes that Blau intentionally provides separate paradigms for the two local varieties. The Amêdî and Sincarî charts for “do”, “fall” and “come” are not interchangeable.

## 2. Directly verified: the secondary present stem wer-

On printed p. 96, the note states that Amêdî hat'in/hê- and Sincarî hatin/ê- have a variant present stem **wer-**. It explicitly says that the present, future, subjunctive and imperative are built on this variant stem.

The chart visibly gives the following forms:

| Person | Present | Subjunctive |
|---|---|---|
| ez | werim | werim |
| tu | werî | werî |
| ew (3sg) | were | were |
| em | werîn | werîn |
| hûn/hing (2pl) | werin | werin |
| ew (3pl) | werin | werin |

Imperative forms shown:
- 2sg: **were**
- 2pl: **werin**

The future column places the future marker variants **-ê / dê / wê** before the corresponding wer- forms. This is a stem-based paradigm: the future marker is not part of the lexical stem itself.

**Important boundary:** the note presents wer- as a variant present stem for the two locality-specific forms of “come”; it does not say that all verbs with a similar meaning use wer-, nor that wer- replaces hê-/ê- in every tense. The printed note specifically associates wer- with present, future, subjunctive and imperative.

## 3. What the other charts do and do not establish

The visual chart pages confirm the existence and locality labelling of the full conjugation tables:
- kirin/ke- (Amêdî) and kirin/k- (Sincarî), printed pp. 90–91;
- k'eft'in/k'ev- (Amêdî) and k'etin/k'ev- (Sincarî), printed pp. 92–93;
- hat'in/hê- (Amêdî) and hatin/ê- (Sincarî), printed pp. 94–95.

The charts include present, future, present imperfect, past imperfect, preterite, two perfects, pluperfect, present subjunctive, perfect subjunctive, perfect conditional and past conditional columns. However, because the table typography is rotated and some diacritics and endings are small, this addendum does **not** publish a reconstructed full paradigm transcription for all six charts. Each cell would need a separate high-resolution transcription and independent check before learner use.

In particular, do not normalize the source spellings (for example, apostrophes and vowel marks) silently into Hawar orthography. Retain the source form and any editorial normalization in separate fields.

## 4. Pedagogical and linguistic implications

1. A verb should be documented by its principal parts and attested stem variants, not by a rule that assumes the present stem can always be predicted from the infinitive or past stem.
2. The Amêdî and Sincarî paradigms should be kept in separate columns and tagged with locality.
3. The wer- chart is useful evidence for stem alternation and for the construction of future/subjunctive forms, but it is not a universal Standard Kurmanji paradigm.
4. These historical forms are not approved replacements for the Academy's proposed Standard Kurmanji/Hawar-based pathway. Any dialect-comparison resource requires explicit editorial approval and a native-speaker review.
5. Existing lessons and seed data remain unchanged.
