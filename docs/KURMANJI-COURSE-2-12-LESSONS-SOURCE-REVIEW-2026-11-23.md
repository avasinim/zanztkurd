## Cross-lesson quality findings
1. The current lesson bodies are primarily cautious explanatory paragraphs and methodological warnings; several lessons lack concrete examples, practice items, or visible citations. They are structurally present, but they are not yet a fully source-anchored teaching set.
2. Source references such as “Haig (2018), Table 11/14, §2.7.1” are present in seed content, but citation details should be made consistent, clickable, and auditable in the displayed lessons.
3. Dialect scope needs to be explicit. The course says Standard Kurmanji, but some claims should not be generalized across regional varieties without qualification.
4. Lessons 02, 03, 06, 07, 08, 10, and 11 would benefit from short, source-verified paradigms and example sentences plus gloss/translation, not only abstract prose.
5. The course should preserve the “do not invent examples” rule: only add examples after they are traced to a published source or approved author-provided material.
