# Blau (1975): Catalogue, Holdings and Primary-Text Access Audit — 2026-11-17

## Purpose

This addendum advances the previous locality-evidence audit by checking whether the 1975 primary source can be located through catalogues and whether its contents are actually available online. It does not extract or teach grammatical forms.

## Verified catalogue and access findings

1. **Google Books record.** The record identifies Joyce Blau, *Le Kurde de Amadiya et de Djabal Sindjar*, C. Klincksieck, 1975, series volume 8, ISBN 9782252017975. It reports 252 pages, says the source copy originated at the University of Michigan and was digitized on 28 July 2006, but the current page states “No eBook available.” The existence of a digitization record must therefore not be represented as an accessible full-text scan.
2. **Zheen Library OPAC record.** Search-indexed catalogue metadata identifies the same title, author and 1975 publication, records 253 pages, and lists a copy as held in the public library of the Zheen Foundation. The record describes the item as printed text and identifies its components as introduction, notes, bibliography, phonology, analysis, folk texts and glossaries. This is a promising physical-holdings lead, not proof that the book can currently be borrowed, scanned or delivered.
3. **Bibliothèque nationale de France.** The BnF catalogue lists Blau's 1975 work under two title-author catalogue entries. This independently corroborates the bibliographic identity and offers another institutional catalogue route; the record alone does not establish local availability.
4. **2025 scholarly biography.** Martin van Bruinessen's article in *Kurdish Studies Journal* 3(1), pp. 109–126, is available as an open-access PDF. Page 117 states the 1967 Amadiya and 1968 Sinjar field trips and says the field notes became the basis of the 1975 dissertation. Page 118 characterizes the book as field linguistics based on recorded oral narratives, transcription, analysis of each dialect and translations, and notes substantial differences from standard Kurmanji.

## Page-count discrepancy

The Google Books record reports 252 pages; the Zheen Library OPAC reports 253 pages. Do not silently choose one figure. This may reflect catalogue conventions, inclusion of unnumbered pages or a record-level discrepancy. Resolve it against the physical book's title page, pagination and final numbered page when a copy is inspected. Until then cite the book without a page-total claim, or note both catalogue values.

## Next acquisition workflow

1. Request a holdings/circulation check from Zheen Library using the exact title and ISBN above; ask whether supervised consultation or lawful reproduction of selected pages is possible.
2. In parallel, query the BnF and relevant university catalogues for a consultable copy or interlibrary-loan route.
3. If a copy is obtained, first photograph/scan the title page, contents, pagination and bibliography; then index the phonology, analysis, oral texts and glossaries separately.
4. For each linguistic claim, preserve the exact page, locality, text/speaker context, original transcription and translation. Do not infer a full paradigm from a glossary stem or one narrative token.
5. Keep Amadiya and Sinjar evidence in separate locality columns and have a qualified native editor familiar with each locality review any proposed learner-facing interpretation.
