# K-078 Free-Speech Future Evidence and Questionnaire Cross-Check Boundary

Date: 2026-11-17  
Type: source-method audit; documentation only.

## Question

Can the K-078 free-speech future examples be independently cross-checked against the University of Manchester questionnaire/database records for the same Şemdinli speaker?

## What the source pages establish

1. The University of Manchester sample page identifies the recording as **K-078 Şemdinli, Turkey**, provides an audio link, and publishes the transcription and English translation. In the free-speech transcript, turns 7, 11, and 14–17 contain multiple occurrences of *dê* and the negative clause *em ceyranê girê nadeyn*.
2. The Manchester database index places K-078 under **Turkey (Kurmanji)** and describes the database as searchable by sample code, location, grammatical tag, English questionnaire phrase, or Kurdish text. It says the data derive from transcriptions of recorded interviews with speakers from identified locations.
3. The K-078 page itself links to sample-specific questionnaire/map entries:
   - 3.9.3 “3SG inflection, future: ‘Tomorrow it will rain’”
   - 3.11.1 “Future tense marker: ‘Tomorrow it will rain’”
   - 3.11.2 “Future tense marker: ‘Tomorrow I will go to town’”
   These are relevant targets for a same-speaker cross-check, but the linked map pages are interactive map shells; the accessible page text does not expose the K-078 point's recorded Kurdish response.
4. The ArcGIS map link opens an embedded interactive map, but the retrieved text does not expose its feature attributes or the selected K-078 record. Therefore the exact questionnaire response for K-078 has **not** been independently recovered in this audit.

## Important distinction: two evidence types

| Evidence | What it can support | What it cannot support by itself |
|---|---|---|
| K-078 free-speech transcript and audio | Contextual use by this speaker in a connected narrative; relation between *dê*, reported plans and future reference | A full elicited paradigm or a general rule for all Şemzînan speakers |
| Linked questionnaire maps | Potentially controlled comparison of the same speaker's responses to future-tense prompts, if the K-078 feature record is retrieved | Exact K-078 form while only the interactive map shell is accessible |
| Database location index | Sample identity and regional grouping | Generalization to all speakers in the locality |

## Methodological caution

The Manchester project's fieldwork description distinguishes questionnaire elicitation from free-speech narration. Questionnaire prompts were translated orally by speakers after being read in a second language; recordings were transcribed. The extended project also asked speakers for short narrations. Therefore, even when the same consultant contributes to both, a questionnaire response and a spontaneous narrative are not interchangeable evidence types.

The free-speech corpus is a selected and transcribed portion of a longer recording. Published methodological discussion notes that free-speech samples are extracts and that the corpus represents only part of the recorded material. Do not describe the available transcript as the speaker's complete speech corpus.

## Next verifiable research step

To complete a genuine cross-check, retrieve the feature attributes for K-078 from the interactive map or the database query interface for each linked future prompt. Record:
- the exact Kurdish response as displayed;
- whether the response is tagged as 3SG future inflection or future tense marker;
- whether the sample identifier is explicitly K-078;
- the map/query metadata and any transliteration conventions.

Until those attributes are available, the safe status is **cross-check identified, response not yet retrieved**. Do not invent a response from the free-speech examples or infer the questionnaire form from the map title.

## Current safe conclusion

The free-speech evidence already documents a range of *dê*-marked future-oriented contexts and one contextually future-translated negative clause without overt *dê*. The same speaker's questionnaire future records are discoverable as linked targets, but their exact forms remain unverified. This is a concrete research lead, not yet an independent corroboration.
