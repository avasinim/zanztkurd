# Xwendin: past-tense negation and object-agreement audit

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only audit for the Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway.

## Executive finding

A directly attested teaching source gives the following negative past-transitive contrast for **xwendin** (“read”):

- **Min helbest nexwend.** — “I did not read the poem.”
- **Min helbest nexwendin.** — “I did not read the poems.”

The contrast preserves the singular/plural object agreement pattern in negative clauses:
- **nexwend**: negative prefix **ne-** + past stem **xwend**, with third-person singular object (zero ending).
- **nexwendin**: negative prefix **ne-** + past stem **xwend** + plural agreement **-in**, with plural direct object.

The surface spelling joins the prefix to the stem: **ne + xwend → nexwend**. The plural suffix remains visible at the end: **ne + xwend + in → nexwendin**.

## 1. Directly attested xwendin examples

*Kurmanji Kurdish, Lesson 7* (lesson handout, section “Negative, transitive and intransitive verbs”) explicitly gives:
- **Min helbest nexwend.** — “I did not read the poem.”
- **Min helbest nexwendin.** — “I did not read the poems.”

The same lesson explains that the negative marker **ne-** is prefixed and that the other past-transitive rules remain in force. Thus the object-agreement contrast seen in affirmative clauses remains visible under negation.

## 2. Morphological analysis

| Form | Segmentation | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **nexwend** | **ne- + xwend- + Ø** | negative past; singular third-person object |
| **nexwendin** | **ne- + xwend- + -in** | negative past; plural third-person object |

Here **Ø** means no overt third-person singular agreement ending. The **-in** on the plural form is the past agreement marker associated with the plural direct object, not a marker of the oblique agent **min**.

This analysis is supported by the lesson's paired negative examples and its general explanation of object agreement in past transitive clauses.

## 3. Compare affirmative and negative pairs

| Object number | Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| Singular | **Min helbest xwend.** | **Min helbest nexwend.** |
| Plural | **Me ew helbest xwendin.** | **Min helbest nexwendin.** |

The affirmative singular/plural pair is also documented by Thackston (§18.3): **Me ew helbest xwend / Me ew helbest xwendin**. The negative pair is directly attested in Lesson 7. The agent differs between these source pairs, so do not treat the table as a fully controlled minimal quartet; its purpose is to compare the attested morphology.

## 4. Word order and scope

For the simple past negative transitive form documented here:
1. **ne-** is the negative prefix.
2. The past stem of **xwendin** is **xwend**.
3. The past transitive agreement ending, when required by a plural direct object, remains at the end of the verb form.
4. The agent remains oblique (**min**, **me**, etc.); negation does not change the ergative case frame.

Do not insert **di-** into these simple past forms. Past progressive negation is a separate construction and should be audited separately if it is to be included in the curriculum.

## 5. Teaching-safe decision

For the Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway:
- Teach **nexwend / nexwendin** as the directly attested singular/plural negative contrast.
- Explain **ne-** as the negative prefix and **-in** as plural object agreement in the plural example.
- Keep the agent case and object agreement explanations separate.
- Do not derive a full person-by-person negative paradigm from these two examples alone.
- Do not generalize this exact spelling or prefix behavior to every dialect without checking dialect-specific sources.
- Do not modify learner-facing lessons until explicit approval.
