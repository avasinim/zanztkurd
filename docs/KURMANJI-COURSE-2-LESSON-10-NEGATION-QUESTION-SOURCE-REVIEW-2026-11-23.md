## Negation findings — verified
Haig (2018), §2.6.1, pp. 125–127 supports these core points for Standard Kurmanjî:
- Present-stem forms require a prefix slot: indicative `di-`, subjunctive `bi-`, or negation `na-` (indicative negation) / `ne-` (subjunctive negation). The negation prefix replaces the corresponding indicative/subjunctive prefix.
- The verbs `zanîn` (know) and `karîn` (be able) use `ni-` in present negation: `nizanim` / `nikarim`.
- Simple past and past progressive negation use `ne-`: `ne-hatin` (“they didn’t come”) and `ne-di-hatin` (“they weren’t coming”).
- Standard Kurmanjî examples: `Ez şîr ve-na-xw-im` “I do not drink milk”; `nizanim` “I don’t know”; `nikarim` “I cannot”.
- The chapter explicitly notes regional variation; Western Kurmanjî past imperfective negation and other dialect-specific patterns must not be generalized to Standard K.

Lesson seed comparison:
- The seed correctly notes `na-` replacing `di-` in many present indicative forms, and provides `nizanim` and `nikarim`.
- The seed’s past contrast `ne-hatin` / `ne-di-hatin` is source-supported.
- **Confirmed terminology defect:** heading “نەفیی ڕستەی ناوەکی” has no defined term or explanation in the reviewed content. It is not supported as a distinct category by this spot-check.
