# Xwarin / xwarandin: transitivity, feeding meaning, and stem-evidence audit

**Date:** 2026-11-17  
**Scope:** Documentation-only lexical and morphological audit for the Standard Northern Kurmanji pathway.

## Executive finding

The pair must not be described as a simple intransitive-to-transitive factitive pair:

- **xwarin** is already a transitive verb meaning “to eat”. Haig & Öpengin list its past stem as **xwar-** and present stem as **-xw-**. Wiktionary independently gives the same stems and attests forms such as **ez dixwim** and **min xwar**.
- **xwarandin** is a separate transitive verb, listed by Kurmanji Wiktionary with the meaning **xurekkirin** (“to feed / provide food”), derived etymologically from **xwar + -andin**. The dictionary page does not provide a conjugation paradigm or direct present/past stem table.
- The semantic relation is “eat” → “feed / cause to eat”, but because **xwarin** is already transitive, this is a valency-increasing / causative extension, not the elementary intransitive → transitive pattern illustrated by **mirin → mirandin**.
- Do not teach **xwarîn-** or **xwarand-** as confirmed stems for *xwarandin* until a direct paradigm source is found.

## 1. Base verb: xwarin

Haig & Öpengin's Northern Kurdish overview lists:
- Infinitive: **xwarin**
- Past stem: **xwar-**
- Present stem: **-xw-**
- Meaning: “eat”

The Kurmanji Wiktionary entry independently marks **xwarin** as transitive and supplies:
- Present stem: **-xw-**
- Present forms: **ez dixwim**, **tu dixwî**, **ew dixwe**, **em/hûn/ew dixwin**
- Past stem: **-xwar-**
- Past example: **min xwar**

This is direct evidence that the base verb is already transitive. Its object may be food (e.g. **nan**) or another contextually licensed object; the lexicon also records extended senses beyond literal eating.

## 2. Derived verb: xwarandin

The Kurmanji Wiktionary page **xwarandin**:
- labels it transitive;
- gives the lexical gloss **xurekkirin** (“to feed”);
- gives the etymological segmentation **xwar + -andin**;
- places it in the Kurmanji **-andin** verb category.

The page does **not** provide:
- a present stem;
- a past stem;
- finite present examples;
- a finite past example;
- an imperative paradigm;
- person/object agreement forms.

Accordingly, this audit treats **xwarandin** as a lexically attested transitive verb meaning “feed”, but does not manufacture a full conjugation from the suffix alone.

## 3. Morphological interpretation

Thackston's *Kurmanji Kurdish: A Reference Grammar* (§28) describes the regular factitive pattern as deriving a transitive verb from an intransitive base using **-andin**, with factitive present stems in **-în-**. Its examples include **mirin → mirandin** and **tirsîn → tirsandin**.

That rule is not a complete substitute for a lexical paradigm:
- **xwarin** is not intransitive; it is already transitive.
- **xwarandin** is attested as “feed”, a meaning that can introduce a feeder and a person/animal being fed, and may therefore have a more complex argument structure.
- It is unsafe to infer that the present stem must be **xwarîn-**, or that the past stem must be **xwarand-**, without a directly attested finite form or a reliable conjugation table.

The derivational segmentation **xwar + -andin** is supported by the lexical entry; the exact finite stem alternations remain unverified in the sources consulted.

## 5. Teaching-safe decision

For Standard Northern Kurmanji learner content:
1. Teach **xwarin** as “eat”, transitive, with present stem **-xw-** and past stem **xwar-**.
2. Teach **xwarandin** as a separately attested transitive verb meaning “feed” only if the lesson needs that lexical item.
3. Do not present the pair as a basic intransitive → transitive factitive model.
4. Do not publish a present/past paradigm for **xwarandin** until a source with direct finite forms is located.
5. Keep **xwarin** (verb “eat”) distinct from **xwarin** (noun “food / meal”), both of which are listed in the same dictionary entry.
